## Question

Can the three exact physical corona types outside L18 occur in any whole-plane
fixed-chirality edge-to-edge straight-Spectre tiling, without assuming a
parent partition or substitution ancestry?

## Exact method

Seed corona indices `33`, `44` and `155`. For every surviving patch, enumerate
every set of translated/rotated straight Spectres that covers each exposed
unit edge exactly once and has no interior overlap with the patch or with
another new tile. Repeat on the complete frontier. The only inputs are the
exact 14-edge polygon and the fixed-chirality edge-to-edge contact convention.

The ring constraints contain no parent templates, child slots, colored states,
substitution labels or generated-patch filter; the generated control is used
only to identify which three exact physical types are outside L18. The
independent verifier builds a separate one-hot CNF and blocks full assignments
rather than reusing the producer's enumeration loop.

The earlier physical prefix proved that only 21 of 166 first-corona types can
reach radius three, of which 18 are L18 and the remaining three are exactly
the roots above. Therefore every complete corona in any whole-plane tiling in
the declared edge-to-edge domain belongs to L18.

## Consequence and boundary

Composed with sessions 48 and 53, D1/D2/D3/D5/D6 are verified throughout the
fixed-chirality edge-to-edge domain: every tiling enters L18, has the unique
8/9 parent partition, and contracts to the 17 generated colored states.

This is not yet the unrestricted straight-Spectre theorem. We still need a
local geometric reduction excluding T-junctions or other non-edge-to-edge
contacts, and D4 must connect every representation faithfully. C1 all-level
legality and C3 inradius growth remain separate blockers.

unrestricted edge-patch bridge through combinatorially equivalent aligned
hat–turtle tilings. The targeted audit is recorded in

machine certificate with that theorem: the finite correspondence from its
maximal-segment edge patches to our 14 primitive module contacts must first be
encoded and checked.
