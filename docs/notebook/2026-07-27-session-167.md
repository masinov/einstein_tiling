# Session 167 — HC-40 hinge orbit parity

**Date:** 2026-07-27

**Checkpoint:** `HC-2026-07-27-40`, session 1 of 3

## Objective

Run ERR-016's sector/half-plane test first on the proposed three-occurrence
reflection hinge.  If a symmetry-free carrier forbids a singleton third
occurrence, derive the minimum reflection-invariant replacement exactly.

## Result

Theory note 53 proves:

- K45O: reflection acts freely on occurrences in any invariant local star of
  a symmetry-free carrier, so every occurrence orbit has size two;
- N49: an invariant three-occurrence hinge is impossible;
- K45H: the minimum hinge has four occurrences in two reflected pairs.  The
  mirror axis consists of two opposite contact rays and the sector sequence
  is `alpha,beta,beta,alpha`, with `alpha+beta=pi`.

The local equations are nonempty, for example
`alpha=pi/3`, `beta=2*pi/3`.  This is a sector-star witness, not a polygon.

## Decision

The literal third-participant proposal is refuted, but the authorization
explicitly admits the forced four-participant correction.  Session 168 must
decide whether this minimal star is state-neutral or can carry exactly two
states once the two mirror-axis rays have distinct intrinsic roots.  No
boundary or coordinate construction is authorized.
