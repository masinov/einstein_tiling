# Session 44 — Kaplan isohedral-surround SAT control

**Date:** 2026-07-20  
**Decision:** D-0052  
**Primary source:** Kaplan, *Detecting Isohedral Polyforms with a SAT Solver*,
EPTCS 403 (2024), 118--122.

## Criterion and implementation

Kaplan's Proposition 1 reduces isohedral tileability to one finite object: a
simply connected 1-patch in which every neighbour is extendable by the same
congruent surround. The new `a1_isohedral.py` implementation uses:

- marked exact D6+translation poses;
- exact cover of the central tile's halo;
- pairwise placement conflicts;
- inverse-neighbour clauses;
- direct composition-conflict clauses implementing extendability;
- lazy hole cuts;
- an independent positive-certificate verifier.

A1 and this filter answer different questions. Isohedral SAT is complete for
one-transitivity-class tilings in the alignment model. A1 searches bounded
translation quotients and therefore also finds periodic anisohedral tilings.

## Falsification and correction

The first version used only edge-neighbour halo cells. Its complete seven-kite
count was 54, contradicting Myers's 52. Covering edge neighbours does not
necessarily cover every angular sector at a vertex, so the centre need not be
in the topological interior of the proposed 1-patch.

Replacing it with A2's full vertex halo produced:

```text
n                  1  2  3  4  5   6   7   8
our isohedral      1  1  4  4  0  70  52  37
Myers isohedral   1  1  4  4  0  70  52  37
```

This is exact agreement across all 1,264 free polykites through order eight.
