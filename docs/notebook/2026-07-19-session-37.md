## Exact affine-circuit oracle

The V4 potential variables can be eliminated without SAT. Each selected tile
adds equations `p(v) XOR p(u) = c` in `GF(2)^2`; a packed XOR union-find checks
compatibility and deletion-minimizes an inconsistent placement set. Translation
of such a set gives its complete quotient orbit. Unit tests compare this oracle
against the original CNF on deterministic random subsets and pin a minimal
incompatible triple with all 16 translates on the 4x4 quotient.

The ordinary no-good-clause LP is too weak. On 4x4 it sees 12 packing-circuit
orbits and 378 affine pair-circuit orbits, but its symmetric bound is 6
placements per center rather than `1/2`. This failure is structural: a size-`s`
no-good has incidence `s` and right-hand side `s-1`, so nonnegative sums of
ordinary circuit clauses cannot by themselves reach the desired ratio.

## Adaptive two-center matching

Every area-ten placement touches exactly four substrate centers. This suggests
an adaptive certificate: assign each selected tile two distinct incident
centers, with center capacity one. Hall's theorem would give

```text
2 * selected tiles <= distinct substrate centers.
```

The trusted implementation is a small augmenting-path matcher, not a graph
library. On failure it returns an explicit tile subset `S` and its exact
neighborhood `N(S)` with `|N(S)| < 2|S|`. Exhaustive random controls compare it
against all subsets of small incidence systems. The known 4x10 density-half
extremizer has a perfect 40-edge two-matching.

### Essential torus/plane distinction

A direct 4x4 torus CEGAR search produced a compatible eight-placement set with
a seven-tile `13 < 14` Hall witness. This does **not** refute the planar idea:
the quotient identifies four centers across its seams. The same seven literal
placements lifted to the plane touch 17 centers and have a saturated matching;
all eight touch 20. The counterexample is retained as a regression test against
using small-torus Hall data as planar geometry.

Interior planar-window searches avoid that identification. The 2x2 and 3x3
windows are UNSAT using only the singled-out packing pairs and affine-incompatible
pairs. A direct HiGHS integer model on the 4x4 planar window has optimum
`2|S|-|N(S)| = 0`. These are strong finite controls for the planar conjecture,
not a proof for arbitrary finite sets.

## Local rational-dual search

Write `d(c)` for the number of selected tiles incident to center `c`. Hall is
equivalent to nonnegative total charge `sum_c (2-d(c))`. A finite taper
`phi(c)` would prove the bulk bound if every compatible local configuration
satisfied

```text
sum_c phi(c) d(c) <= 2 sum_c phi(c).
```

Summing every translate of one such inequality counts each tile four times
per unit taper mass and yields density at most `1/2`. The cutting-plane program
therefore alternates:

1. a continuous minimax master for `phi`; and
2. an exact weighted compatible-packing separator, refined by whole
   translation orbits of deletion-minimal affine circuits.

For the seven-center radius-one region the method terminates negatively. Both
nonnegative tapers and signed tapers with `|phi| <= 8` have minimum local bound

```text
42/13 = 2 + 16/13 > 2.
```

Thus no radius-one certificate of this form exists. The nonnegative artifact
records 168 incident placements, 3,966 pair conflicts, 127 learned translated
affine clauses, and the finite separating configuration list. It is a
reproducible discovery/minimax payload; an exact rational cold verifier is
still required before promoting the `42/13` value to a machine-verified
negative theorem.

Radius two has 19 centers, 360 incident placements and 9,468 pair conflicts.
It exposes genuinely new affine circuits (sizes 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 11 were
observed) beyond the radius-one seed library. Its nonnegative master lower
bound had already risen above `0.62` after ten separating configurations,
strong evidence that finite nonnegative tapers retain a boundary obstruction;
the run was deliberately stopped before completion to test signed discharge.
Signed radius one converged to the same `42/13` obstruction.

Two persistent exact continuations were launched at the session cut:

`w2_d7_taper_r2` (radius-two dual, seeded from the saved radius-one orbit

## Status and next theorem move

T2.D7 remains a conjecture. What is now established cleanly is the right
combinatorial reformulation and several falsification gates:

- small-torus Hall matching is unsound for the plane because of seam
  identifications;
- ordinary circuit-clause LPs are structurally too weak;
- fixed local marking and radius-one tapered/signed discharge are insufficient;
- the planar two-matching statement survives exact windows through 4x4.

The next proof-producing target is the planar sparsity theorem itself:
pairwise packing plus shared V4 compatibility should imply that the four-center
incidence hypergraph is `(2,0)`-sparse. A proof may be expressed as a binary
matroid representation or as a finite reduction of minimal Hall circuits.
Larger taper radii remain useful diagnostics, but should not replace that
structural theorem.
