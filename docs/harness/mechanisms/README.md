# Research-mechanism registry

The registry describes the small number of controls that govern transitions
from thought to commitment, evidence, or public claim. It is not a workflow
engine and does not prescribe how mathematics should be invented.

An active mechanism must state:

- the historical failure it prevents;
- the boundary where it acts;
- what it requires or forbids;
- why free exploration remains outside its scope; and
- the frozen drift cases that exercise it.

New mechanisms begin as `proposed`. Activation requires a human-reviewed
change to the registry and a replay against the drift corpus. Generated
mechanisms may never rewrite this registry or activate themselves at runtime.
