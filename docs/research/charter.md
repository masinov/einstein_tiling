# Research charter: connected unmarked planar Einstein tiles

**Status:** current research authority
**Adopted:** 2026-07-30

## Mission

The project studies connected unmarked planar tiles that tile the Euclidean
plane but admit no periodic tiling. Its purpose is to make progress on the
field-level problem, not to maximize the number of locally verifiable results.

Meaningful successes include:

1. constructing and proving a genuinely new Einstein tile or family;
2. proving necessary or sufficient conditions for a broad, recognizable class
   of connected unmarked tiles;
3. proving nonexistence or a classification theorem for such a class;
4. determining an undecidable realization boundary together with meaningful
   decidable subfamilies; or
5. creating a certified discovery or verification method that materially
   changes how one of the preceding questions can be attacked.

The current Sturmian realization problem is an important program within this
mission. It is not the definition of the whole project, and the reconstructed
Akiyama--Hamada--Ito system is a benchmark source rather than a required
architecture.

## The unrestricted-tilings standard

Einstein-hood quantifies over every tiling admitted by one unmarked support.
An intended patch, encoded local state, large finite disk, or failed bounded
period search does not establish aperiodicity.

A constructive result must ultimately establish:

- exact support and at least one whole-plane tiling;
- the declared Euclidean isometry convention, including reflections when
  allowed;
- the complete relevant contact language rather than only intended contacts;
- a total all-tilings argument, such as forced hierarchy or a finite-radius
  map to an aperiodic system; and
- immediate rejection if an exact periodic tiling certificate is found.

## Three research modes

### 1. Free exploration

Exploration may be speculative. It includes conjectures, examples, analogies,
alternative definitions, proof sketches, counterexample attempts, and links to
other areas of mathematics. It does not require a formal proposal or a claim
of theorem distance.

Exploratory material is non-authoritative. It cannot promote a tile, claim
novelty, launch a nontrivial computation campaign, or silently become the
research agenda.

### 2. Sustained research program

An exploratory idea becomes a sustained program when it has:

- a coherent mathematical thesis;
- a plausible connection to one of the mission-level outcomes;
- a declared scope or realization class;
- a statement of what evidence would refute or redirect it; and
- a comparison with higher-level alternatives in the portfolio.

Once admitted, ordinary proof development inside the program remains fluid.
Individual definitions and lemmas do not require separate authorization.

### 3. Evidence and integration

Strict admission applies when work crosses into nontrivial computation,
candidate promotion, canonical theorem integration, novelty claims, or public
research conclusions. Exact arithmetic, primary-source checks, independent
verification, fail-closed outcomes, and reproducible provenance apply at this
boundary.

## Generality and examples

The repository uses the following descriptive scope ladder:

```text
field-level problem
architecture-independent class
recognized mathematical family
specific construction
single instance
```

The ladder is not a ban on examples. Peculiar examples often reveal the right
theorem. It is a guard against allowing a convenient example to become the
agenda without an explicit strategic decision.

After substantial instance-level work, the program must ask whether the work
has produced a general conjecture, reusable invariant, construction mechanism,
counterexample, or recognized class. If it has not, the result remains a case
study and the next direction is selected again from the whole portfolio.

## Strategy selection

The next research direction is selected from the program portfolio, not from
the final paragraph of the latest working note. Selection is qualitative and
considers:

- mathematical significance;
- breadth of the possible result;
- uncertainty that can genuinely be reduced;
- relationship to current literature;
- availability of exact proof or certificate routes;
- risk of entering an indefinitely extensible family ladder; and
- promising connections to independent areas of mathematics.

There is no scalar progress score. Lemma counts, session counts, eliminated
solver cells, artifact size, and model-estimated progress are not substitutes
for mathematical judgment.

## Bilevel improvement of the research method

The harness itself may be studied when the research trace exposes a systematic
failure such as repetition, local hill-climbing, prior-art neglect, problem
misinterpretation, or disagreement between policy and execution.

Harness changes are proposed and evaluated against preserved historical drift
cases. They are reviewed, versioned, and deliberately activated. No agent may
rewrite and inject a live research mechanism merely because generated code
imports or a shallow metric improves.

The mechanism review is event-driven, not periodic. Its role is to repair the
research process, not continuously score or constrain mathematical thought.

## Provenance without bureaucracy

Failed programs and useful negative results are preserved, but routine
maintenance and every disposable thought do not need numbered sessions. The
canonical theory integrates what survives; workspaces retain useful unfinished
reasoning; the archive preserves the historical trace.

The project gates commitment and promotion, not creativity.
