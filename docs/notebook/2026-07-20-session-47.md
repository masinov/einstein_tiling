**Date:** 2026-07-20

**Inputs:** the ancestry-blind `166→30→21` physical central-corona prefix and

## Question

Do the three locally extendable physical corona types 33, 44 and 155 survive
when parent choices must agree as one partial exact partition across
overlapping tile neighborhoods?

## Buffered semantics

A Boolean parent variable denotes one exact translated/rotated occurrence of
the recovered 8- or 9-child template. Parent selection and exact physical
next-ring completion are coupled in one CaDiCaL instance.

For a fixed inner patch, a tile is a required target only when every parent
occurrence still compatible with that visible geometry lies in the union of
the patch and all legal next-ring tile candidates. Such universally buffered
tiles must be covered by exactly one selected parent. Every other visible or
candidate tile is covered at most once.

This avoids forcing the artificial physical boundary to coincide with a
parent boundary. The implementation guard found three radius-two branches of
corona 44 and twelve of corona 155 with no buffered target. They were expanded
by unrestricted physical exact cover rather than counted as grouping UNSAT.
All fifteen have no physical third-ring successor, so the final result is
unchanged for a sound reason.

## Controls

Every one of the 18 corona types observed in the level-3/4 substitution
controls was located in an exact generated graph-radius-four ball. All 18
admit independently checked buffered parent partitions. A representative
control has 64 physical tiles, 35 required buffered tiles, eight candidate
parent groups and a valid four-parent partial partition.

## Exact claim boundary

The computation proves that coronas 33, 44 and 155 cannot occur in a
whole-plane tiling **that admits the recovered 9/8 parent partition**. It does
not prove that every geometrically admitted physical tiling admits that
partition. Nor does it prove uniqueness or that the contracted parents belong
to the same all-tilings domain. These are precisely the remaining D2, D3 and
D5 obligations in the Chéritat route.

W3 stays `VALID_PARTIAL_CERTIFICATE`; C5 gains a sharper conditional finite
closure but remains partial.
