**Date:** 2026-07-22

**Checkpoint:** `HC-2026-07-22-15`, session 2 of at most 3

**Mode:** on-paper exact contact-complex mathematics; no experiment

## Proposition

Count the intrinsic states carried by a fully occupied host side partitioned
into unequal whole neighbor sides under full Euclidean isometry, and derive
the exact local angle constraint at every internal T-junction.

## Prior art and non-redundancy

Session 100 established that edge patches and T-junction atlases are prior
art. The new bounded question is only the order capacity of the fixed
subdivision-word class. No audited source was used to claim novelty for the
calculation.

## Result

K6O proves that `k` pairwise distinct neighbor roles have exactly `k!/2`
order classes, because a host-interval isometry only preserves or reverses
order. N20 therefore kills the tempting three-occurrence binary gadget:
two unequal neighbors give one full-isometry state, not two.

The first surviving cases are stronger than expected. Three neighbors give
three states; four give twelve, exactly the capacity needed by the retained
twelve-state source topology. J0 proves the endpoint equation
`rho_i+ell_j=pi` at each ordinary internal T-junction and identifies
right-angled or common complementary endpoint pairs as the natural universal
port vocabulary.

These are interval-contact results only. No congruent polygon, complete
contact atlas, interface propagation or whole-plane tiling is claimed.
