## Mathematical result

The new note `docs/theory/10_stm1_limit_language.md` proves O0. A locally
finite FLC auxiliary patch family whose positive-area overlaps are disjoint
topological disks can be blown down by contracting each overlap disk to a
decorated vertex. The quotient remains a plane, patch interiors become
disjoint, and participant identities plus cyclic order recover the complete
pre-contraction incidence.

Normalized tiny Sturmian triangles have diameter `O(1/kappa)`, so their
geometric limit implements this contraction. Their decorations preserve the
auxiliary BD-coordination data without pretending the physical tiles
overlapped.
