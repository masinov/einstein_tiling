## Environment and scope

The environment contains no SymPy, python-flint/FLINT, Sage or PARI interface.
Rather than silently adding a dependency, Layer C begins with an exact modular
cokernel certificate. It is a strict partial implementation of SNF logic:
positive witnesses prove integer infeasibility, while absence has no polarity.

## GF(2) certificate

For a quotient incidence matrix M, the producer finds a bit vector w satisfying
`wᵀM=0` and `wᵀ1=1`. The compact certificate records the weighted cell support.
The verifier reconstructs every TorusInstance placement and checks even
intersection with every placement plus odd total support.

The gate passes all 60,477 materialized verified periodic certificates with
zero false exclusions.

## Finalist kill table

All 742 HNFs at the area-admissible indices 5,10,...,60 were tested. GF(2)
kills 36: exactly three HNFs at every index. The other 706 are
`not-killed-mod2`, which means unknown—not tileable.

## Infinite-family structure

For the thin quotient HNF `(1,0,k)`, every legal placement is a translate of
one of two explicit ten-cell profiles. A closed mod-2 support formula, with
separate even/odd forms, has odd total weight and even intersection with both
profiles at every translation.

This gives proof draft T2.C1: the finalist cannot tile HNF `(1,0,k)` for any
`k≥4`. In particular it kills an infinite family of area-admissible quotients
`k=5m`. The formula is pinned and independently reconstructed for every
`4≤k≤100`; the theorem itself follows from the uniform two-profile parity
calculation, not extrapolation from that finite test.
