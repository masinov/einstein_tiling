solve every extra-centered colored star exactly. The minimum costs are:

```text

2fb5d0cf9a68ffe2 -> 0 (generated-only star exists)
2f1d7f0fac5b9704 -> 1 (minimum witness contains 288091b49587a4b2)
```

Thus one defect can disappear at one local star, whereas the other two must
propagate at least one step and minimally propagate into each other. This is
the first structure not visible in the uncolored or one-sided alphabets.
