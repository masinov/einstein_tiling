# Session 60 — 2026-07-21 — E1 benchmark go/no-go

## Scope

Determine, from existing artifacts and primary-source comparisons only,
whether the blind Hat/Turtle funnel supports a quantitative classified-corpus
benchmark. No runner, search, SAT job or ablation was launched.

## Sources and evidence reviewed

- Hat paper's `n<=24` classification and Hat/Turtle identities;
- Kaplan's Heesch paper and public polykite implementation/artifact;
- Kaplan's isohedral SAT paper;
- Kaplan's author-review description of tandem Heesch/isohedral ladders and
  the later large polykite search;
- E1 sessions 12--17, frozen artifacts and current runner semantics.

## Result

The exact component validations are strong, including complete A0 counts,
60,477 cold-verified periodic certificates, 116/116 Kaplan `n=8` reproduction,
unique Hat isolation at `n=8` depth three, and Turtle survival of the complete
selected ten-shape `n=10/n=12` bracket.

The historical process is not a clean quantitative benchmark. Hat labels and
behavior informed development; Turtle was an expected named control; budgets
and thresholds adapted after observations; and no full-corpus score exists
because A3/A4 processed only the smallest ten survivors. No matched baselines
or ablations exist. Global rank, recall and false-positive claims would be
invented after the fact.

## Decision

No-go on a discovery-method benchmark or new ablation campaign. Keep a small
reproduction suite and write the exact history as a research-governance
postmortem. The complete reasoning and hypothetical clean protocol are in
`docs/benchmarks/E1_CLASSIFIED_CORPUS_ASSESSMENT.md`.

This is session +3 under `HC-2026-07-21-01`. The human checkpoint limit is now
reached. No further numbered research session may begin until the user reviews
the decision summary and explicitly authorizes a new checkpoint.
