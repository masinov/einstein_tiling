# Session 19 — 2026-07-17 — Crown continuability correction

## The user's concern

"Crown gaps" meant crevices in the last one or two rings that make the finite
patch impossible to continue without overlap. It did not mean an uncovered
kite cell inside the requested disk.

The previous audit answered the wrong question. Zero missing disk cells proves
coverage only. Because every radius had been solved independently, it said
nothing about whether the displayed crown belonged to any larger tiling.

## Verified nested chain

Despite the dead crowns, one growing protected core was constructed:

1. From phase seed 1 at r²=12,800, freeze all 1,576 placements wholly inside
   r²=9,000. They occur unchanged in a verified 9,235-tile r²=50,000 patch.
2. The complete r²=50,000 crown cannot extend even to 60,000. Freeze instead
   all 5,317 placements wholly inside r²=30,000. They occur unchanged in a
   verified 18,386-tile r²=100,000 patch.

This is a literal nested sequence, not three independent circle covers. Its
protected radius grows from √9000 ≈ 94.9 to √30000 ≈ 173.2 while the outer
radius grows to √100000 ≈ 316.2.

The final nested 18,386-tile patch independently retains rank 4 and symmetry
6 at 2048² under the matched-random-null A4 pipeline. Thus the spectral signal
does not depend on the earlier non-nested outer crown.

## Corrected interpretation

- The user's objection invalidates the old use of complete disk patches as
  continuation evidence.
- The candidate is not removed: a nontrivial and growing nested core exists.
- Confidence is lower than the earlier 9,239-tile headline implied because
  the sacrificial collar is deep and solution-dependent.
- Future A3 runs must optimize retained-core growth directly and report collar
  depth. Independent disk tile counts become diagnostics only.
