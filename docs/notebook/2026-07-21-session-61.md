## Result

Go for theorem design, no-go for immediate construction or computation. The
Sturmian result is a finite colored/decorated tile-set theorem. The optimized
`sqrt(2)-1` example has three disk-like prototiles and positive entropy. No
audited general theorem converts it to one shape-only Euclidean tile:
matching-rule geometry preserves types, atlas reduction retains an external
rule, and poly-`K` groupification preserves cardinality/changes setting.

The surviving proposition ST-M1 asks for one unmarked disk whose every tiling
locally and equivariantly decodes to the irrational `sqrt(2)-1` Sturmian
system. Aperiodicity then follows by period descent. The hard obligations are
congruence encoding and a proof that no spurious shape-only tiling exists.
