# Session 42 — literature system and novelty protocol

**Date:** 2026-07-20  
**Decision:** D-0050

## Motivation

Session 40 proved that the supposed E1 finalist was the published Turtle.
Session 41 corrected the `Tile(a,b)` family and finite-census baseline. The
process failure was broader than one missing alias: literature knowledge was
not represented as a versioned dependency of candidate promotion.

## Source audit and local corpus

Eighteen records were entered in `docs/literature/SOURCES.json`, with evidence
class and repository review depth stored separately. Seventeen catalogued open
PDFs were fetched into ignored local storage and extracted to text:

```text
PDFs:   17
bytes:  94,192,706
text:   17 extracts
lock:   data/literature/library-lock.json (SHA-256 per PDF)
```

The PDFs are not staged or committed. `scripts/fetch_literature.py` rebuilds
the local corpus from tracked metadata.

One bibliographic correction emerged during verification. The 2026 paper
`Aperiodic Tile Sets from Sturmian Lattices` (arXiv:2607.14693) is a seven-page
announcement that explicitly points to the full technical paper `Sturmian
Lattices and Aperiodic Tile Sets` (arXiv:2506.19362, revised 2026-01-14). The
catalog keeps both and links the announcement to its technical parent; the
full paper is the implementation target.

The previously omitted SAT paper `Detecting Isohedral Polyforms with a SAT
Solver` was verified as arXiv:2406.16407 / EPTCS 403 (2024), DOI
10.4204/EPTCS.403.25.

## Integration products

- `METHODS_MATRIX.md`: published method to repository component, status, and

- `READING_QUEUE.md`: prioritized full-text and reproduction tasks so
  abstract verification cannot masquerade as a completed audit.
- `tests/test_literature_catalog.py`: catalog structure, identity, parent
  links, mandatory controls, and review-document routing.

## Research consequence

The Turtle work remains valuable as a positive-control result: the blind
funnel isolated a known difficult tile and generated exact finite
certificates. It has not yet recovered the Golden Hex/Sturmian structure,
recognized the Hat--Turtle shared tiling language, or internally completed an
all-tilings aperiodicity proof. Those are now explicit, testable method goals.

New-shape and new-system novelty are separate. Future searches must name the
known search/classification they avoid repeating and must emit the novelty
dossier before promotion.
