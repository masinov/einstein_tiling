# Session 97 — HC-14 single-tile simulation audit

**Date:** 2026-07-22

**Checkpoint:** `HC-2026-07-22-14`, session 1 of at most 3

**Mode:** primary-source audit; no experiment or geometry synthesis

## Proposition

Determine whether K5C's selector, wire, cycle or one-support compilation ideas
are already present in primary literature, and isolate the exact theorem-level
residue before attempting boundary geometry.

## Sources checked

The four mandatory targets were checked in full primary text:
Greenfeld--Tao, Ollinger, Socolar--Taylor plus Ammann A2, and
Lagae--Kari--Dutre.  The audit also checked Fletcher's atlas reduction,
Demaine et al.'s one-puzzle-piece simulation, and Mampusti--Whittaker's
dendrite rule because they are closer to K5C's endpoint or graph mechanism.
The review records theorem locations, scope boundaries and cached hashes.

## Result

The symbolic architecture is not novel.  Ollinger already concatenates all
Wang codes in one meat component, selects one by a jaw, propagates four colors
by wires, and proves every polyomino tiling decodes.  Demaine et al. go further
to one rotatable polygon, but require a prescribed lattice/adjacency model and
leave gaps.  Greenfeld--Tao concatenate finite tiling equations by an auxiliary
finite fibre.  The marked/atlas/corner literature covers the remaining generic
recodings.

No audited theorem gives one connected unmarked planar polygon that covers
the plane exactly and whose every unrestricted tiling decodes.  That exact
geometric erasure step survives; the K5C trie itself does not.

## Decision

HC-14 may spend its two remaining sessions on one exact boundary mechanism
for K5C.1--K5C.3.  Replacing the trie, delimiter, wires or corner relation by
another symbolic version is forbidden.  If finite cycles, pre-decoding state
visibility and contact completeness cannot all be proved, the cyclic-corridor
route closes without enumeration.

No runner, experiment, SVG, polygon or generated artifact was produced.
