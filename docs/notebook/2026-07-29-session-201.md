## Finding

The finite-automaton contact compiler is not a novelty claim. Its symbolic
content is standard finite-state/matching-rule machinery, and its prescribed
three-participant topology is already within published edge-patch formalisms.
The exact angle assignment is useful as a compact repository normal form.

The audited sources do not close the one-connected-unmarked, unrestricted
gapless, total-decoder problem. Their retained hypotheses include labels,
atlases, several supports, gaps, prescribed lattice sites, and auxiliary
fibres. This is a dated absence report, not evidence of novelty or
nonexistence.
