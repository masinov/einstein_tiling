# Session 180 — translation-periodicity gate for the source-native envelopes

## Experiment pre-registration

### Proposition

Decide whether either exact 49- or 51-rhombus common support transcribed from
AHI Figure 45 is a fundamental domain for a triangular-lattice translation
sublattice.  Enumerate every Hermite-normal-form sublattice of the forced
index (49 or 51) and test whether the support contains exactly one primitive
triangle of each orientation in every quotient coset.

### Prior art and non-redundancy

The criterion is the standard finite quotient characterization of a lattice
fundamental domain.  It is used here only as the mandatory exact periodicity
gate for two newly transcribed fixed supports.  No claim of novelty attaches
to the criterion or enumeration.

### Outcome decisions

- A quotient bijection is a complete periodic tiling certificate and forbids
  the bare common support from being an aperiodic monotile or carrying a total
  decoder to the aperiodic source.
- If none exists, record only absence of a translation tiling in this exact
  lattice-preserving class.  Do not infer aperiodicity and do not enlarge the
  periodic search.

### Stop rule and finite justification

For a support of `2N` primitive triangles, a one-tile translation fundamental
domain must have lattice index `N`.  Enumerate exactly the finite HNF list at
that index, test quotient injectivity separately for up/down triangles, and
stop.  No multi-tile periods, rotations, reflections, SAT, or bounded-period
escalation.

all 57 HNF sublattices fail.  Neither support is a one-tile translation
fundamental domain on the normalized triangular lattice.
