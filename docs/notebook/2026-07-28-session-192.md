# Session 192 — all-area carrier trade reduction

## Done

- Derived K66A, the complete arithmetic phase diagram for every carrier area
  `A=15q+s`.
- Proved K66T: every carrier-local compiler at any area contains a finite
  same-support AHI trade changing the number of large macros, with exactly 15
  singleton cells exchanged per large macro.
- Recast the source corridor rule as K66C: `M` long diagonals are precisely a
  cut of a binary vertex potential, so the local existence problem is a
  count-changing cut-admissible exact cover plus the remaining AHI vertex/SAB
  constraints.
- Stated N66R, the all-area closure criterion.  No area-31 enumeration was
  opened.

## Verified

- The slope sequence `A/k-15` is strictly decreasing and the irrational cut
  lies between `floor(A/(15+r))` and its successor.
- The admitted bands through area 90 follow exactly from `s/q<r`, with
  `r=6(sqrt(2)-1)`.
- Equal carrier area gives the exact exchange law
  `Delta M=-15 Delta large`.
- The corridor-cut equivalence is the standard cycle-parity/integrated-
  potential theorem on each finite graph component; K64B is its all-singleton
  specialization.

## Failures / boundaries

- No conserved boundary charge has yet been proved.  Same geometric support
  alone cannot provide one because each large macro is geometrically
  dissectible into 15 common rhombi; source corridor and vertex data are
  load-bearing.
- A contextual carrier may decode different marked boundary data in different
  states, so a charge depending on fixed marked boundary colors would not by
  itself close the unmarked carrier family.
- K66T is a necessary reduction, not a monotile construction and not an
  undecidability theorem.

## Open

Decide the fixed count-changing trade problem by a source height invariant,
an exact witness, or a sharp computability theorem.  This single question now
replaces the unbounded area ladder.
