# Session 194 — coupled vertex algebra and boundary-active reduction

## Done

- Derived K68V, the exact three-rail mixedness code
  `mu=(b xor c,c xor a,a xor b)`.
- Proved that its three nonzero vectors generate the complete even-sum
  sublattice of `Z^3`; the synchronized `(6,6,6)` defect is therefore not
  excluded by any additive local vertex charge.
- Proved N68H: no globally admissible, same-support, complete-marked-boundary
  pair can change its number of large source macros, at any carrier area.
- Combined N68H with K66T, N63R and K63F to isolate the only surviving
  carrier-local class: boundary-active, joint multi-rail contextual phase
  transport.

## Verified

- Source Definition 4 makes each cyclic rectangular-cell role the ordered
  pair of two transverse rail bits; N60V is exactly the forbidden `111`
  mixedness star.
- Genuine source phase triples witness every one of the four mixedness types
  at `alpha=sqrt(2)-1`; the comparison with `q=2-sqrt(2)` was kept exact.
- The determinant of `(110),(101),(011)` has absolute value two, proving the
  claimed integer lattice rather than merely parity inclusion.
- A changed indexed gap is readable on cells arbitrarily far along its
  unbounded strip, so two complete source configurations that differ only in
  a bounded set have identical rail words.
- K67D then turns equality of the corridor field into equality of large-macro
  count on the common support.

## Failures / boundaries

- The hoped-for stronger local coupled charge does not exist: the local
  algebra admits the synchronized even defect.
- N68H does not close K66T's contextual definition, because it deliberately
  permits two carrier states to expose different marked boundary data.
- No exact contextual phase-transport relation, unmarked polygon, whole-plane
  tiling, or aperiodicity theorem was constructed.
- No novelty claim is made for the parity and propagation arguments.
