**Date:** 2026-07-20

## Question

Does the radius-five D1 theorem cover every fixed-chirality straight-Spectre
tiling, including polygonal tilings with vertices on sides and apparent
T-junctions, or only the primitive edge-to-edge contact model?

## Exact boundary hypotheses

The raw `Tile(1,1)` direction word has 14 unit edges, uses all twelve
30-degree directions, and merges into thirteen maximal polygon sides:

```text
primitive length 1: 12 sides
primitive length 2:  1 side
```

The length-two side is the pair of consecutive direction-6 edges separated by
the existing 180-degree boundary vertex. Every interior angle is at least 90
degrees. No maximal side has 90-degree corners at both endpoints.

## Two-side bound

At a junction between distinct sides along one half-plane, the two incident
tile sectors must fill 180 degrees. Since all angles are at least 90 degrees,
both are exactly 90 degrees and no additional point-only tile fits. A sequence
of three sides would therefore give its middle tile a side with right angles
at both ends, contradicting the checked boundary. Every maximal straight
interface has at most two polygon sides on either side.

## Finite correspondence

The one-side length words are

```text
(1), (2), (1,1), (1,2), (2,1), (2,2).
```

Equating total lengths on the two sides gives exactly ten ordered patterns,
of total primitive length one through four. Splitting every length-two side at
its collinear primitive vertex gives offsets `0,1,...,N` on both sides in all
ten cases. The asymmetric corner offsets are precisely the apparent
T-junctions, so each is already a primitive boundary vertex. Forgetting the
artificial 180-degree subdivisions is the inverse map.

Any adjacent copies share a boundary direction, forcing their relative
orientation to be a multiple of 30 degrees. Connectivity propagates one frame
through the tiling. Matched primitive endpoints are exact module points, so
all tile anchors lie in the rank-four module after fixing one root anchor.

Both even/odd edge-length deformations close exactly, remain simple, and have
the expected exact squared lengths. This independently cross-checks the
published Theorem 3.1 route through aligned Hat–Turtle tilings.

## Consequence and boundary

Every unrestricted fixed-chirality straight-Spectre tiling is represented by
the exact primitive model used in session 54. Composing the ten-case bridge,
the radius-five L18 entry theorem, the unique parent transducer and contraction
closure verifies D1/D2/D3/D5/D6 throughout the full fixed-chirality hull.

This does not cover mixed handedness, which is outside the Spectre motion
convention. It also does not finish the aperiodicity certificate: D4 faithful
equivalence and D7 period descent remain open, together with C1 and C3.
