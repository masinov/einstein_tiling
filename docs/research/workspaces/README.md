# Problem-centered research workspaces

A workspace keeps a coherent mathematical investigation together without
turning every work unit into a numbered session. A typical workspace may use:

```text
problem.md
research_notes.md
proof_draft.md
counterexamples.md
references.bib
```

The files are working artifacts rather than canonical theory. They may record
failed approaches, uncertainties, and competing interpretations. Results move
to `docs/theory/` only after their statement, scope, dependencies, evidence,
and prior-art status have been reviewed.

Workspaces are sequential by default. Independent criticism may be requested
at meaningful proof or interpretation boundaries; the repository does not
assume parallel research threads.
