# Session 187 — marked Sturmian undecidability and the unmarked boundary

## Goal-level question

Replace the impossible request for a finite case list of every possible
Sturmian polygon by a rigorous classification boundary: either a general
construction theorem, or an undecidability theorem with meaningful decidable
subfamilies, without confusing marked and unmarked monotiles.

## Theorem U2

Fix a finite Wang presentation `Y` of the nonempty aperiodic AHI source.  For
arbitrary Wang `W`, form `Y x W` and apply Stade's effective
Wang-to-AB-to-marked-stick construction.  The resulting support is one
connected polygon with a finite edge matching relation, and every valid
tiling locally factors to `Y`.  It tiles exactly when `W` tiles.  Hence this
marked geometric Sturmian realization problem is undecidable.

Every nonempty output is a marked aperiodic monotile by period descent.  This
is a standard product corollary of the primary theorem, not a novelty claim.

## Decidable subfamilies D3

- If `W` is the row-constant Wang shift built from a finite directed graph,
  existence is equivalent to a directed cycle.
- If `W` is the local periodic lift of a supplied width-`w` cylinder, existence
  is equivalent to a cycle in its finite row-transfer graph.
- On the unmarked carrier-local side, N60C remains a decidable algebraic
  rejection test; P17 is the completed source-specific instance.

## Boundary and decision

The theorem does not establish undecidability for one unmarked polygon.
Stade geometrizes the finite edge rules with a second noncongruent staple.
Consequently the exact live theorem is self-stapling color erasure: make the
same connected support enforce the edge relation with a total all-tilings
decoder, or prove this impossible for an explicitly defined self-stapling
family.  No new isolated carrier is admitted.
