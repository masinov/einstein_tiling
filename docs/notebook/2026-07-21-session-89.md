## Result

N10 proves that the undeformed convex K3F kite has only its standard
three-copy equilateral macrotiling. It therefore has no contextual state.

K3R identifies the constructive alternative: seek one polygon with several
exact retilings of one forced macrocell. Retiling choice and boundary
subdivision become symbols. A conditional theorem separates exact retiling,
unique macro grouping, complete contacts, source decoding, chirality and one
lift. This is a compiler-aware inverse problem rather than blind polygon
enumeration.

The full-edge deterministic case is also closed. With one uniquely aligned
neighbor per complete side, the rooted flag star is fixed and periodic. A
viable carrier must use multiple exact macro retilings, discrete alternative
alignments, or explicitly classified contact subdivisions.
