## Question

## Safety criterion

For a quotient `q` and radius `r`, the adversarial domain is the full local
closure `Z(q,r)`: every configuration all of whose radius-`r` patches occurs
in the intended quotient image. Intended images alone are insufficient,
because a geometric carrier enforces finite local compatibility and may admit
spurious recombinations.

ST-M1.Q0 states the sufficient condition: `Z(q,r)` must be nonempty and admit
a total finite-radius equivariant decoder into an irrational-only Sturmian
system. Period descent then excludes every nonzero period.

## Natural quotient refuted

Forgetting macro ownership, cell addresses, internal completion ports and the
forced two-half `M` component leaves the unrestricted `S/M/L` cabinet roles
with SAB continuation. Source Remark 7 explicitly says `{S,M,L}` forms
Sturmian lattices with every slope. A rational slope gives a periodic
configuration, so this local-rule system fails Q0.

This refutes the natural three-role quotient, not every conceivable
three-symbol recoding.

## Second no-go

Keeping three finite-state corridor languages but removing their
two-dimensional macro coupling falls under ST-M1.N1. Each nonempty
one-dimensional sofic rail has a periodic point; compatible independent
periodic rails give a periodic plane configuration.

Thus any safe quotient must retain either the `2S+L`/`M` macro coupling or a
different genuinely two-dimensional mechanism that still has a total
irrational-corridor decoder.

## Remaining safe presentation

The full S0 addressed/collared alphabet remains safe. Its uncollared core has
38 addresses, but the source and repository do not yet contain the complete
finite collar/port table. State names can be merged only after proving
future-equivalence: identical compatible exterior/lift behavior and identical
virtual corridor output. Radius-one appearance, degree, or frequency does not
establish this.

The next possible symbolic deliverable is therefore the explicit table—not a
polygon:

4. overlap well-definedness and a total decoder on the quotient local closure.
