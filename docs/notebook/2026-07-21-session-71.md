## Three-coset calculation

For indexed lines `a_r,b_j,c_k`, put `s=r+j+k`. The normalized limiting
centroid is

`g(r,j,k)=(j-s/3,k-s/3)`.

An isometric cell `H^a_{j,k}` has four generating triangles of orders
`-1,0,0,1`, so its vertices lie in

`Z^2+(1/3,1/3)`, `Z^2`, and `Z^2-(1/3,1/3)`.

These cosets are disjoint. A vertex therefore determines its order `s`, its
indices `j,k`, and then `r=s-j-k`. Distinct physical source vertices do not
coalesce.

The marked split produces primitive edge differences `(2,-1)/3`,
`(-1,2)/3`, and `(1,1)/3`, up to symmetry. No vertex of the three cosets lies
inside one of those edges, so no T-junction or new physical edge crossing
appears.
