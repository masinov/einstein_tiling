## Results

1. **N39.** Same-polarity long spokes are impossible. Each has horizontal
   displacement greater than `L=sqrt(v^2-u^2)`. Fitting two eastward or two
   westward such segments into width `v` forces the intervening `b,u` path
   to shift horizontally by more than `2L-v`. But host closure and N38 give

   ```text
   b+u < sqrt(v^2+u^2)-(3/sqrt(2)-1)u < 2L-v.
   ```

2. **K29O.** Only the two opposite-polarity cells remain. There `X<0` for
   `R=X+iY`, and the complete K27X closed-intersection conjunction is
   equivalent to the single inequality

   ```text
   A*Y-B*X <= 0.
   ```

   Thus simplicity is exactly `A*Y-B*X>0` for this pair.
3. The twelve HC-30 critical cells collapse to two: `(r_b east,r_c west)`
   and `(r_b west,r_c east)`, each carrying N38 and the K29O strict safe
   inequality. They retain all 117 other segment predicates, containment and
