# Integrated paper and monograph outline

This outline is an assembly map, not a claim that every chapter is complete.
The proof ledger controls which statements may appear as theorems.

## Part I — Finite local rules on the kite substrate

1. The kite complex, exact coordinates, and \(\Lambda\rtimes D_6\).
2. Polykites, placement anchors, and finite-type encoding.
3. Tiling spaces, stabilizers, and scope conventions.
4. Periodic completion (T0.1) and the collapse of weak/strong translational
   aperiodicity.
5. Compactness and the distinction between finite witnesses and uniform
   existence theorems.

Primary sources: `01_periodic_completion.md`, roadmap §§1–2.

## Part II — Exact periodicity machinery

6. HNF torus quotients and exact-cover certificates.
7. Cylinder transfer graphs and per-vector decisions.
8. Cycle reconstruction, cycle-free certificates, and semilinear width sets.
9. Counting, character, and Smith-normal-form obstructions.
10. Nonabelian boundary words, binary-coupled torus holonomy, and DRAT core
    certificates.

Primary sources: `W1_TRANSFER_SPEC.md`, `02_transfer_certificates.md`,
`03_w2_invariants.md`, `04_w2_cokernel.md`, `05_w2_binary_holonomy.md`,
roadmap §§3–4, A1 implementation.

## Part III — Existence and recognizable hierarchy

## Part VI — Turtle-control postmortem

23. Blind rediscovery history, delayed identity check, and
    crown-continuability correction.

This part is a research-governance and validation case study. It must name the

## Part VII — Arithmetic symbolic factors beyond the polykite catalog

28. Quadratic Sturmian lattices and finite colored source systems.
29. The equal-support proof draft: composition, limiting incidence, and the
    source decoder.
30. Full-local-closure safety and period descent through total local maps.
31. Distributed contact codes, parity arity, and hidden-state lower bounds.
32. Gauge-invariant boundary holonomy and sector-separated vertex states.
33. Full-isometry semantics, chirality, and the conditional K2J carrier
    theorem.
34. Reproducibility boundary SER0 and the blocked geometric realization.
35. Minimal aperiodicity versus surjective positive-entropy factors.
36. Retiling compilers: synchronization markers, interface-arity no-gos, and
    the conditional closed-corridor selector.

Primary sources: `07_stm1_sturmian_monotile_design.md` through
`19_synchronizing_retiling_topology.md`, with note 14 as the consolidated
symbolic reading path and notes 15--19 as the geometric/compiler branch,
and `docs/literature/reviews/STURMIAN_MONOTILE_ENCODING.md`. The symbolic
chain is an internal proof draft. It is not a monotile construction: SER0
lacks extensional source tables and K2J lacks an unmarked geometric carrier.
