## Full-text result

The proof has two independent halves. Golden Hex patch-tiles plus Golden
Sturmian gap patches give arbitrarily large inballs and hence tilability.
Dispensable Golden Ammann bars are then forced in every Turtle tiling; their
generalized lines form a Kagome grid, their crossings identify flipped tiles,
and a double length count forces irrational bar frequency. The second half
does not assume that arbitrary Turtle tilings arise from the Golden Hex
substitution.

The theorem/lemma map and exact claim boundary are recorded in
`docs/literature/reviews/AKIYAMA_ARAKI_TURTLE.md`.
