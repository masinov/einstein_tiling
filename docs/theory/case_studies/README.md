# Mathematical case studies

Case studies retain exact specialized mathematics that is too narrow to be a
general theorem but remains useful as an application, obstruction template,
counterexample, or future paper component.

- [AHI realization](ahi_realization.md) is the dependency-oriented closure of
  the selected Section 10.1 program.
- [Geometric carriers](geometric_carriers.md) integrates the failed carrier
  families and extracts their transferable geometric lemmas.

Full chronological derivations are preserved under
`docs/archive/theory_sources/` and indexed by
`../reference/SOURCE_MAP.json`.
