## Question

single-unmarked-tile proof architecture before any geometry is attempted,
including the full-Euclidean-group and mixed-handedness cases?

## Source correction

The full Akiyama--Hamada--Ito source distinguishes two facts that must not be
merged:

- Section 10.1 gives a three-prototile system and proves positive topological
  entropy by propagated interchangeable patches.
- The end of Section 10.2 says that at formal `kappa=infinity` the cell
  supports no longer differ and the number of patch tiles *may* be reduced to
  one up to color.

The latter is a strategy remark, not a complete equal-support colored
construction or a bridge to the positive-entropy system. Theorem 4 itself
constructs an aperiodic finite tile set for quadratic irrational slope and
Remark 7 explicitly leaves the monotile possibility open.

## Verified conclusion

The design does not yet justify drawing or searching for a polygon. Two
upstream mathematical objects are missing:

1. **ST-M1.S0:** a complete finite colored equal-support source system, with
   an all-tilings irrational-Sturmian theorem, derived rather than inferred
   from the `kappa=infinity` remark;
2. **ST-M1.K1:** a finite coupled contact-star kernel expressing at least
   three states with a total unambiguous decoder and the actual
   three-direction intersection relation.

Without S0 there is no rigorously defined equal-support target. Without K1,
“encode colors by boundary teeth” is only a slogan and supplies no
no-spurious-tilings lemma. The branch is therefore blocked before geometry,
not promoted to an experiment.

## Failures and limits

- No complete color/contact alphabet was extractable merely from the source's
  `kappa=infinity` paragraph.
- No proof was found that the equal-support proposal is a factor of, or
  surjects onto, the Section 10.1 positive-entropy system.
- The coupled contact-star architecture is conditional. No geometric
  realization theorem for its required finite atlas has been proved.
- ST-M1.N1 applies only under its explicit product and compatibility
  hypotheses; it does not rule out coupled finite-state encodings.
- ST-M1.C0 is a local-map reduction theorem, not the missing construction.
