## What “candidate” means now

These are the first genuinely new shape candidates in the project. They were
obtained from the complete blind n≤16 enumeration and are not the hat or
variants of a hat hierarchy.

They are not yet einstein or quasicrystal candidates. A three-corona witness
is local-growth evidence only, and the prevalence at n=16 shows that raw depth
is not comparable across sizes. A3 large-patch growth and A4 diffraction must
provide the next separation.
