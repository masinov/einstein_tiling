# Session 177 — exact Turtle-style edge-substitution compiler

## Experiment pre-registration

### Proposition

Decide whether the exact `large_A` and `large_B` source supports can become
one congruent simple polygon under the direct Turtle-style geometric color
erasure: replace every unit boundary edge independently by the two segments
through the center of either the incident rhombus (inward) or its reflected
outside mate (outward), while the four inward choices collapse the singleton
`M` rhombus to its center-spoke graph.  Enumerate the complete finite sign
spaces, canonicalize under the full lattice isometry group, and serialize
every common simple-support class.

### Prior art and non-redundancy

Smith's rhombic derivation of the Hat/Turtle family explicitly eliminates its
black hole rhombus by this two-segment edge substitution.  The AHI source has
the analogous large-patch/singleton structure, but neither paper applies the
construction to the Section 10.1 `sqrt(2)-1` system.  This is an exact test of
that published mechanism on the fixed reconstructed source, not a free polygon
or polykite search.

### Outcome decisions

- A common class is only a *carrier support*.  Preserve its exact sign words
  and immediately test source contact compatibility, periodicity, and the
  all-tilings decoder before any candidate claim.
- No common class proves impossibility for the complete unit-apex,
  independently signed, single-notch edge-substitution family.  It does not
  rule out longer edge words, partial contacts, or other carrier mechanisms.

### Stop rule and finite justification

Enumerate each binary boundary word exactly once.  Retain only simple cycles,
use integer triangular-lattice coordinates throughout, and compare exact
full-isometry canonical forms.  Do not enlarge the notch vocabulary or search
other supports in this run.

## Results

The source's patch-edge convention subdivides both published outlines into 16
unit cell edges (rather than the 14 and 13 maximal polygon sides visible in the
outline).  The exhaustive spaces are therefore `2^16` and `2^16`, a strict
superset of the initially stated word counts.

The correct rhomb-center substitution makes the all-inward singleton `M`
boundary a zero-signed-area center-spoke walk.  Of all boundary sign words,
20,736 for `large_A` and 15,552 for `large_B` give simple polygons.  Their
full-isometry canonical-support sets are disjoint: the number of common
simple supports is exactly zero.

An initial implementation mistakenly routed through the adjacent equilateral
triangle apex rather than the incident rhombus center.  That diagnostic did
not collapse `M` and is rejected as the wrong geometric family.  The final
artifact and verifier use the center-spoke construction stated above.

## Decision

The direct Turtle/James-Smith center-spoke color-erasure mechanism is
impossible for the fixed AHI large supports, even with independently chosen
inward/outward polarity on every unit patch edge.  This closes that entire
finite deformation family.  It does not close longer edge words, contextual
partial contacts, or a cell-level carrier enforcing K53E.
