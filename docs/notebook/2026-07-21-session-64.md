- The paper does not list a common-cell subdivision, transported color

Thus the source suggests S0 but does not state or prove it.

## Mathematical result

The new note `docs/theory/08_stm1_equal_support_compiler.md` proves
ST-M1.S0C. A finite system of connected macrotiles over one congruent periodic
cell, with finite exposed-edge rules and a common frame, is MLD with a finite
colored tile set having only that cell support.

The construction colors each cell by macro type, handedness, internal address,
unique internal ports, and exposed boundary/SAB data. Internal connectivity
forces a unique complete macro component; grouping and subdivision are inverse
bounded-radius maps. Periods are therefore preserved both ways.

The proof does not cover disconnected macro supports without additional
coordination data, which is why the connected Section 10.1 control was chosen.

## Remaining exact lemma

ST-M1.E-infinity must construct a nondegenerate common cell for the three
Section 10.1 templates and prove:

1. every constituent support is congruent;
2. all transported macro templates remain finite and connected;
3. the complete SAB/boundary language transports bijectively;
4. the irrational Sturmian symbolic sequences survive equidistancing.

The fourth item is essential: after metric long/short differences are erased,
their symbolic distinction must remain in the colors. Otherwise the result is
only a periodic trigonal cellulation.

visible macro shapes.
