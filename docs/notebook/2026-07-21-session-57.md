## New prior-art correction

Kaplan's peer-reviewed Heesch paper was not the end of the relevant record.
His public `heesch-sat` repository explicitly supports polykites. His official
project page reports non-tiling polykite computations through roughly 16 or
17 cells and links a 116-page `8kites.pdf` artifact.

The PDF's SHA-256 is
`8e710b8d9418ca5ab6d4510fb6dba36080eac980166e1b355ed491e6304e8f12`.
Its aggregate is `104 x (H_c=1,H_h=1)`,
`4 x (H_c=1,H_h=2)`, `5 x (H_c=2,H_h=2)`, and three inconclusive cases.
Kaplan identifies the latter as two periodic anisohedral shapes and the Hat.
That exactly matches the repository's `720/108/5/Hat` A1+A2 partition at
eight kites.

Conclusion: A2 is an independent exact reproduction and useful benchmark,
not a novel census. ERR-005 and the detailed source/code crosswalk record the
correction.
