## Exact translation audit

`(Δu,Δv)=(0,47)` throughout the central r²=16,000 comparison:

- 706 eligible labeled tile placements;
- 706 exact translated matches.

No second independent translation is exact there. The match falls below one
farther outward, so this is a stripe/approximant domain, not an exact period
of the full disk.

Exact torus cylinders forced to contain this vector were tested at HNF
`(a,0,47)` for `a=5,10,15,20,25` (indices 235 through 1,175). All are UNSAT.

The general exact SAT torus sweep now gives a contiguous no-certificate range
through index 215, with no budget exhaustions. Indices 225, 230, 235, 245 and

remaining HNF-heavy indices became an hour-scale long tail; unreturned
indices are explicitly unclassified. This is an A1 parallelization problem,

## Interpretation

The system was robust about A3 coverage but the first rendering made its
scope visually unclear. A4 also needed a new guard: a single SAT patch can
contain a highly regular domain and must not be trusted alone.

The finalist survives the stronger independent-patch test, but its correct
status remains **high-priority candidate**, not new einstein:

- finite disk patches do not prove a plane tiling;
- bounded torus searches do not prove aperiodicity;
- rank estimates are numerical and symmetry is still size-sensitive.
