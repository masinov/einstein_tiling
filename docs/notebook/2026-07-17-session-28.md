# Session 28 — W2.D binary-coupled torus holonomy

**Date:** 2026-07-17  
**Scope:** implement the Layer-D boundary-network CSP, exhaust the strong S3
quotient classes on the first unresolved admissible shell, and preserve
independently checkable UNSAT certificates.

## Encoding and soundness controls

`holonomy_csp.py` reconstructs every legal torus placement's actual outer
boundary, reduces substrate vertices modulo an HNF while retaining deck
coordinates, and couples placement selection to S3-valued vertex potentials.
The two deck generators take one of the 18 commuting ordered pairs in S3.

Cell coverage is only at-least-one: overlaps are allowed. This is a deliberate
relaxation, so every exact cover remains a model before holonomy constraints
are added. The one-kite tiler passes on three tori, and stored periodic shape
392 passes its nontrivial `(2,0,2)` three-tile quotient in both relaxed and
exact modes.

## Fixed-map cascade through index 60

The phase-0 canonical order-3-kernel map was applied only after the W1
period-family and GF(2) filters. At indices 40,45,50,55,60, the prior stack
left respectively 3, 9, 18, 21, 45 HNFs (96 total). Every placement-only
relaxation was SAT and every coupled instance found a commuting-twist model:
78 on twist 1, 16 on twist 4, and two on twist 11. This is a clean negative
result for one quotient map, not tiling evidence.

## Exhaustive strong S3 classes at index 40

The 234 S3 surjections whose zero-displacement kernel has order three form 39
classes under simultaneous inner conjugacy. All 39 were tested on the three
index-40 HNFs surviving W1:

| HNF | killing classes | placement-only relaxation |
|---|---:|---|
| `(10,3,4)` | 6 | SAT |
| `(40,11,1)` | 6 | SAT |

Thus Layer D contributes genuinely new exact exclusions. The 87 other
index-40 HNFs contain a T1.2-36 impossible period vector. Together with the
area obstruction between admissible shells, every finalist HNF through index
40 is excluded. This remains a finite grid-aligned period-lattice result and
does not prove O1.

## Independent proof gate

CaDiCaL 1.9.5 found the UNSAT cases efficiently, but the PySAT proof stream
omitted a checkable terminal conflict and failed `drat-trim`; it was rejected
as a certificate source. Glucose 4 emitted valid DRAT.

For the lowest killing class of each HNF, all 18 twists were regenerated with
Glucose. `drat-trim` commit `2e3b2dc0ecf938addbd779d42877b6ed69d9a985`
verified each raw proof, extracted a core CNF/core proof, and verified the
trimmed pair. The standalone replay then regenerated the canonical CNF,
checked each core was a multiplicity-respecting clause subset, checked all
compressed and uncompressed hashes, and independently replayed all 54 proofs.
The stored certificate set is 91,143,478 compressed bytes.

A one-CNF disjunction over all 18 twists was also tested. It is logically
clean but expands an index-40 case to 729,994 clauses and took 323.58 seconds
with CaDiCaL, so separate twist cores remain the practical certificate format.
