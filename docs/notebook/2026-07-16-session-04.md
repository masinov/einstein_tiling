# Session 04 — 2026-07-16 — A3 large patches; SAT backend; φ⁴ chirality anchor hit

## Done

1. **A3 module** (`src/einstein/funnel/a3_patch.py`). Task posed as exact
   cover of a disk-shaped cell region (copies may overhang; an enclosed
   empty cell is just an uncovered region cell, so hole-freeness is
   automatic). Two engines:
   - `grow_patch` — pure-Python greedy: most-constrained-first frontier
     (lazy heap, center-out tie-break), fail-first backtracking, escalating
     partial restarts. Kept for the growth-profile feature
     (options-per-decision histogram) and as no-dependency fallback.
   - `sat_grow_patch` — CNF (1 var/placement; at-least-one per region cell;
     pairwise conflicts) → CaDiCaL (`python-sat`, new dependency, D-0009).
     Models decoded and **re-verified by our own exact verifier** — the
     external solver is never trusted. UNSAT with `fix_seed=False` is a
     pose-free refutation: no set of copies covers that disk at all.
2. **Batch driver** `scripts/run_a3.py` (grow / refute / ceiling modes;
   stage `A3-patch`).
3. **Renderer**: `groups_to_svg` (per-placement fills → chirality maps).

## Results

- **Greedy fails on the hat, SAT does not** (measured, kept honest):
  greedy fills r2=500 (~130 hats) in 4–20 s but stalls near ~245 tiles at
  r2=1000 even with 1M nodes — wrong early commitments get buried beyond
  chronological backtracking. Corona-by-corona growth via the A2 engine is
  worse (205 tiles in 362 s). CaDiCaL solves r2=1000 in 0.03 s. This is
  itself evidence of the hat's long-range order (and why the spec
  prescribes SAT).
- **Hat ladder** (verdicts + verified certificates in DB, shape 635):

  | r2 | region cells | tiles | reflected | solver |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 2000 | 3,654 | 488 | 13.52% | 0.07 s |
  | 5500 | 10,002 | 1,311 | 12.89% | 5.7 s |
  | 22000 | 39,870 | 5,103 | 12.89% | 43 s |
  | 50000 | 90,714 | **11,514** | **12.81%** | 220 s |

- **External anchor hit (E-log): chirality density.** The hat paper's
  H7/H8 substitution (arXiv 2303.10798, p.19; compound→{1 compound,
  5 hats}, hat→{1 compound, 6 hats}) has matrix [[1,1],[5,6]] with Perron
  eigenvalue (7+3√5)/2 = φ⁴ (paper states the φ⁴ inflation), and one
  reflected hat per compound ⇒ reflected density 1/(1+φ⁴) ≈ 0.12732 in any
  hat tiling. Our SAT patches converge monotonically to it:
  13.85% (130 tiles) → 13.52% → 12.89% → 12.81% (11,514 tiles). A3's
  output reproduces literature tile statistics it was never told about.
- **The six H_c=2 shapes have hard disk-cover ceilings** (pose-free UNSAT,
  milliseconds each): ids 483/502/1080 refuted at r2=100, ids 238/580/637
  refuted at r2=200 (each covered the previous ladder radius; largest
  patches 19–35 tiles). The hat covers r2=50000 = 500–1000× larger disk
  area. Internal consistency with A2: finite H_c ⇒ bounded patches ⇒ a
  refutation radius must exist, and does, for all six.
- **Periodic control** (shape 392, n=8): 1,319 tiles at r2=5500 in 5 s —
  A4's periodic reference patch.
- Asset: `assets/hat-patch-r2-5500-chirality.svg` (reflected hats dark;
  isolated and evenly dispersed — the textbook hat-tiling picture).

## Failures / dead ends (data)

- v1 greedy grower with plain chronological backtracking + full restarts
  hung at r2=500 with the default budget knobs (restart threshold too
  lax). Fail-first re-push + partial restarts fixed r2=500 but still
  walled at r2≈1000. Root cause is structural (long-range conflicts), not
  tuning — hence the SAT pivot, which the spec prescribed anyway.
- A2's corona engine cannot serve as a patch grower (recomputes
  ring/placements per level; exhausted 3M nodes at depth 7).
