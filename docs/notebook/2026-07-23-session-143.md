## Experiment pre-registration

### Proposition

Decide the two fixed QF_NRA cells left by N39/K29O. Both retain the complete
normalized K21Q formula—positive weights, unit direction charts, all K17S
containment, host closure and all 120 exact nonadjacent-segment predicates—
and add N38 plus exactly one polarity assignment:

- `P_+-`: `r_b` east/north and `r_c` west/south;
- `P_-+`: `r_b` west/south and `r_c` east/north.

Both add K29O's strict safe inequality. The two instances exhaust every
simple K16W point by N39; no same-polarity or extra K27X cell is included.

### Prior art and non-redundancy

Jovanovic--de Moura's NLSAT decision procedure and Z3's QF_NRA implementation
were already audited for K21Q in session 134; this claims no solver novelty.
The earlier monolithic instance reached a resource stop. N38, N39 and K29O
now delete the rest of its parameter space by proof and provide a new exact
two-cell decomposition not previously run. The research object is the fixed
carrier obligation and exact cold verification boundary, not NLSAT.

### Outcome decisions

- A SAT model upgrades a cell only if the independent exact verifier confirms
  the original geometry, all 120 segment predicates, N38, its polarity and
  K29O. Such a model reopens carrier geometry only; it is not a tile,
  placement patch or aperiodicity result.
- Both cells may close K16W only if UNSAT evidence is independently replayable
  as an exact certificate. Bare solver UNSAT is recorded as solver evidence
  and leaves the theorem ledger open.
- `unknown`, resource stop, killed process, unverifiable model or a mixture of
  nonterminal outcomes leaves the surviving cells open/frozen. It does not
  authorize a rerun, alternate ordering, added cell or larger budget.

### Stop rule and finite justification

Run exactly the two named cells sequentially in the printed order `P_+-`,
`P_-+`. Each receives one Z3 5.0 QF_NRA ordering, an internal four-hour
timeout, an external `timeout` supervisor at 14,400 seconds with a 60-second
kill grace, and a 96-GiB resident-memory parameter. Total solver wall budget
is at most eight hours. Preserve each SMT-LIB formula, SHA-256, log/result and
one aggregate manifest. No fallback formula, seed, ordering, partial cell,
weakened simplicity predicate or post hoc budget is allowed.
