# Session 122 — exact K9A semigroup characterization

**Date:** 2026-07-22

**Checkpoint:** `HC-2026-07-22-22`, session 2 of 3

**Mode:** on-paper arithmetic classification; no experiment

## Proposition

Characterize exactly when the transition closure forced by the selected
`ABC/ACB` words accepts no other host word.

## Result

K9A forces every transition into `B,C` and forbids every transition into
`A`. Thus an accepted word is a word over `B,C`, optionally preceded by one
`A`. K13A proves that exactly `ABC,ACB` have host weight `h=a+b+c` iff:

1. `b+c` has only its `(1,1)` representation over `b,c`; and
2. `a+b+c` is not in the numerical semigroup generated by `b,c`.

The first condition is equivalent to neither `b/c` nor `c/b` being a positive
integer. Both conditions are finite exact arithmetic.

This separates the known failures. `(1,2,4)` admits `ABBB` through condition
1; `(1,2,3)` instead admits `BBB` and `CC` through condition 2.
