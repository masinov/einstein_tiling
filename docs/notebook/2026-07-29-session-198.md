# Session 198 — bend the parity zipper into a packing fan

## Done

- Proved K72F, the complete constant-bend family of exact phase zippers.
- Selected `C=5pi/6`, host angle `7pi/6`, with explicit incoming/outgoing
  phase angles and exact mismatch sectors.
- Derived a simple `Q(sqrt(3))` host chain of lengths `4,1,1,1,4` whose
  successive directions turn by `pi/6`.
- Proved K72C, a uniform exterior-port-cone condition sufficient for all four
  parity patches to have pairwise-disjoint congruent occurrences.
- Reduced simultaneous patch packing to one common-polygon cone-embedding
  theorem.

## Verified

- Equal phases give endpoint sum `5pi/6`; mismatches give `3pi/4` and
  `11pi/12`.  Adding host angle `7pi/6` yields respectively
  `2pi`, `23pi/12`, and `25pi/12`.
- The six explicit chain vertices sum the five exact edge vectors.
- The last edge lies strictly below every earlier nonadjacent edge after its
  initial endpoint; the prefix is coordinate-monotone, proving simplicity.
- Word `000` places its three `E_00` occurrences in poses separated by
  `pi/6`, removing the forced-translate geometry of the straight host.

## Failures / boundaries

- K72C is sufficient, not necessary; failure of the cone family would not
  refute interlocking zipper polygons generally.
- No all-contact atlas, source lift, tiling, periodicity result, or monotile
  is claimed.
