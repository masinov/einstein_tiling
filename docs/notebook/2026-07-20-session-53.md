**Date:** 2026-07-20

## Question

Does the typed radius-two defect relation extend, close into a finite defect
cycle, or die when one more complete parent ring is imposed?

## Exhaustive extension

For each of the three surviving radius-two root problems, enumerate every
complete assignment certified in session 52: 960, 432 and 840 assignments.
For each assignment, add every anchor named by its second-ring states and
assign the same 20-state alphabet on the new third ring. Enforce unchanged:

1. reciprocal full/missing endpoint types and exact child-edge contacts;
2. adjacency/nonadjacency agreement for every represented anchor pair;
3. exact nonoverlap of every canonical 8/9-child physical support.

This produces 2,232 finite CSPs with 37–47 represented parent positions.

An independent CaDiCaL encoding replayed all 2,232 instances and reproduced

## Consequence and boundary

Within the fixed-chirality edge-to-edge L18 domain, the already unique 8/9
parent partition contracts only to the 17 generated colored states. This
verifies direct obligation D5 inside L18.

It does not prove that every geometric straight-Spectre tiling belongs to
L18. D1 remains the main recognisability gap. D4 faithful equivalence, C1
all-level geometric legality and C3 inradius growth also remain open, so this
is not yet a standalone aperiodicity proof.

The central substitution-certificate audit now records D2, D3, D5 and D6 as
verified *within L18*, while retaining their weaker global statuses and the
overall `VALID_PARTIAL_CERTIFICATE` verdict. Its independent verifier reports
four remaining global blockers.
