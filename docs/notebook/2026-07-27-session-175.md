# Session 175 — twelve-state corridor quotient

## Experiment pre-registration

### Proposition

Reconstruct, from the pinned Section 10.1 and isometric-cell vector figures,
the local state of every common rhombus as one SAB axis plus the ordered pair
of transverse narrow/wide corridor bits. Decide whether the 31 addresses map
extensionally to the expected 12-state alphabet and serialize its exact
rotation/reflection action.

### Prior art and non-redundancy

The source defines `H^a`, `H^b`, `H^c` from ordered corridor pairs and
defines `S/M/L` by the corresponding width patterns, but publishes no table
for the optimized 31 addresses. Session 172 recovers only role and SAB axis;
the missing ordered `M` subtype is load-bearing for any pose/contact
compiler. This is source transcription, not a new symbolic encoding claim.

### Outcome decisions

- A unique 12-state lift becomes the fixed input to a single-polygon
  two-offset contact construction; macro ownership must then be recovered
  contextually from the exact 31-address templates.
- An ambiguity or smaller/larger alphabet is recorded exactly and blocks
  boundary synthesis until the source state is correctly identified.

### Stop rule and finite justification

Use only the six published isometric cell types and the 31 fixed components.
Classify each once against exact vector/analytic invariants. Do not infer a
subtype visually when two source interpretations survive, and do not search
polygon coordinates.

### Human checkpoint

`HC-2026-07-27-43`, session distance one. The run directly constructs the
source quotient required by the active monotile compiler.

## Results

The published SAB vectors give the exact alphabet
`Z/3 x {0,1} x {0,1}`. `S`, `M`, and `L` are respectively the ordered gap
pairs `00`, `{01,10}`, and `11`; the sign of an M path's 60-degree bend
distinguishes its two ordered subtypes. Both large templates have exactly one
lift of their fixed role-labelled atlas. The singleton M has exactly the two
reflected lifts. All twelve states occur across the three templates.

## Decision

The fixed construction target is K52E: polygon orientation supplies the SAB
axis and two ordered contact stars supply the corridor bits. Pure pose cannot
encode the alphabet because its `D6` action is transitive while the source
alphabet has orbits of sizes `3,6,3`. Macro-totality of this quotient must be
decided before boundary synthesis.
