around every triangular face. Since the plane complex is simply connected,
they sum to zero around every closed path, so the recovered indices are
path-independent. Local gap-equality ports propagate one bit along each
indexed corridor interval. The resulting three bi-infinite sequences and

Internal ports uniquely group the primitive triangles into complete source
macros. The source matching theorem places the decoded cell frequencies on
the Sturmian parabola; P0 intersects the macro-composition segment with that
parabola only at `sqrt(2)-1`. Thus every legal limiting configuration decodes
to the irrational source language and the all-`M` endpoint is excluded.

Flattening any physical source tiling by its indices proves nonemptiness. A
translation period of the colored limit induces a nonzero lattice-index shift
and, by the finite-radius decoder, a period of the irrational source. Hence
the limiting colored system is aperiodic.
