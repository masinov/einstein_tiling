# Shape-only realization theory

[General realization theory](general_theory.md) collects all results whose
hypotheses do not name the AHI source, Turtle, Spectre, or a repository-only
carrier word.  It separates:

1. dynamical sufficiency through total local factors;
2. symbolic and marked undecidability;
3. exact expressive limits of separable contacts;
4. topological constraints on polygon interfaces; and
5. complete finite-state expressivity once a rooted multi-participant contact
   topology is granted.

This separation is the central mathematical conclusion of the consolidation:
finite local relations are easy to compile; forcing their roles and topology
in every tiling by one unmarked support is the unresolved theorem.
