## K2H and N6

Four hidden values `h=(p,q)` suffice. The first side fixes `p` to the parity
of `x`, the second fixes `q` to the parity of `y`, and the third requires the
parity of `z` to be `p+q`. Existentially eliminating `h` gives exactly
`x+y+z=0 mod 2`.

Four are also necessary. Any one hidden value contributes a product box
`A_h*B_h*C_h`. A box contained in even parity must fix the parity of every
coordinate and therefore covers only one of the four even Boolean patterns.
All four patterns occur.

## Geometric boundary

a locally distinguishable pose/contact-star class. A phase obtained only by
integrating edge increments is ambiguous up to a global additive constant;
unless the relations are gauge-invariant or a bounded geometric star fixes
the phase, it smuggles in an external lattice origin and is invalid.

Thus K2G is neither solved nor refuted. Its next exact question is which
finite pose actions of one homochiral carrier can realize four local phase
classes while preserving the triangular frame and G1--G6.
