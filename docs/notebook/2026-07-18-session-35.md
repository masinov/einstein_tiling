## Exact twist-union encoding

Each map-7 HNF has 16 exact-cover-relevant V4 deck-twist pairs. Storing one
proof per pair would require 672 certificate pairs. Instead, the new union
encoder shares placement and two-bit potential variables across all
components, adds one selector per twist, guards each component's implication
clauses with its selector, and requires at least one selector. No at-most-one
condition is needed.

The resulting formula is SAT if and only if at least one direct twist formula
is SAT. SAT and UNSAT controls compare the union polarity with explicit direct
disjunctions. Each index-60 union has 1,456 variables and 645,481 clauses.

## Proof production and cold replay

production, `drat-trim` checked each raw proof, extracted and checked a core,
and the producer confirmed that the core is a multiplicity-respecting subset
of the canonical union CNF. Production used eight workers and retained only
deterministically compressed cores.

A separate cold verifier then:

1. checked manifest completeness for 42 unique HNFs;
2. checked all source and dependency hashes;
3. regenerated each 16-twist union from exact geometry;
4. checked canonical formula and payload hashes;
5. checked every core against the regenerated clause multiset; and
6. replayed all 42 DRAT cores with `drat-trim`.
