## Exact dependencies and criterion

With explicit user authorization, the project environment gained pinned
`sympy==1.14.0` and `python-flint==0.9.0`. For an incidence matrix `M`, the
integer relaxation is feasible exactly when the column lattice of `M` is
unchanged after adjoining the all-ones target. This is decided either from
Smith rank/top determinantal divisors or by equality of canonical row-Hermite
bases after transposition.

SymPy and FLINT Smith forms agree on unit matrices representing rational-rank
failure, same-rank index/torsion failure, and compatibility. They also agree
with FLINT HNF on pinned finalist and periodic controls. Full Smith
diagonalization showed coefficient swell on larger matrices, so the production
census uses the equivalent canonical HNF decision while retaining Smith as the
reference cross-check.

## Interpretation

The full integer relaxation adds no exclusion beyond the GF(2) layer at the
tested horizon. This is a negative discriminator result, not evidence for a
tiling: unrestricted integer solutions may use negative or non-binary
coefficients. The next W2 step must retain positivity/0–1 structure and produce
family-level certificates, or move to nonabelian holonomy. O1 remains open.
