# The direct Turtle center-spoke compiler does not transfer

## N57 — complete finite no-go

Smith's rhombic derivation of the Hat/Turtle family erases its black hole
rhombus by replacing an edge with two segments through the black rhombus
center. Apply the exact analogous operation to the reconstructed AHI source.
For every unit patch edge choose independently either the center of the
incident source rhombus or its reflection across that edge. The all-inward
singleton `M` then collapses to a zero-area center-spoke graph.

Both large supports have sixteen unit patch edges in the source's definition.
Exhausting both `2^16` sign spaces gives

| support | all words | simple polygons |
|---|---:|---:|
| `large_A` | 65,536 | 20,736 |
| `large_B` | 65,536 | 15,552 |

Every simple result was reduced to its irredundant cycle and canonicalized
under translations and the full twelve-element triangular-lattice isometry
group. The two canonical sets have empty intersection.

Therefore no independently signed center-spoke substitution turns these two
source macro supports into one congruent simple polygon while collapsing the
singleton. This includes the literal one-notch mechanism that succeeds for
the Turtle. The statement does not concern longer substituted edge words,
non-frame-preserving deformations, or contextual cell-level compilers.

## Why this matters

The failure is upstream of matching-rule totality: there is no common carrier
support even before testing contacts. The tempting direct transfer of the
published Turtle construction is thus closed completely, rather than left as
another coordinate search.
