## Question

What exactly must a geometric realization of K1P prove, and can pairwise
corner interactions enforce its parity core?

## Result

Theory note 12 defines G1--G6: complete contact/frame normal form, finite mode
extraction, exact star-language equality, source overlap legality, full-
isometry chirality, and one lift. These imply the earlier conditional carrier
theorem. Exact star equality is deliberately stronger than minimal ST-M1;
the distinction prevents N4 from becoming a universal carrier no-go.

The 32-word parity core has full projection onto every pair of coordinates.
Therefore every unary or binary constraint that accepts all legal parity
words is trivial on its scope. No conjunction of side-local and two-side
corner constraints can exclude an odd-parity word. This proves N5.

K2G consequently needs a genuine ternary exact-cover junction, a locally
recoverable auxiliary phase, or a proved larger-radius exclusion. Ordinary
two-edge corner teeth are insufficient.
