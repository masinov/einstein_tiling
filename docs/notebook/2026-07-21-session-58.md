# Session 58 — 2026-07-21 — Kaplan eight-kite coordinate crosswalk

## Experiment pre-registration

### Proposition

Kaplan's 116-page `8kites.pdf` artifact and this repository's complete
eight-kite classifications represent the same 116 individual shapes, not only
the same aggregate counts: 108 with `H_c=1`, five with `H_c=2`, two periodic
anisohedral tilers, and the Hat.

### Prior art and non-redundancy

The controlling sources are Kaplan's peer-reviewed Heesch definition, public
`heesch-sat` kite-grid implementation at commit
`1adb37204013b96b62b954a616265e49d7cf21ad`, the official 2023 PDF and comments,
and the Hat paper's `n<=24` classification. The PDF publishes coordinates and
classifications, so the mathematical data are known. No coordinate-level map
to this repository's `(u,v,s)` canonical keys was located. The only intended
contribution is a reproducible cross-implementation benchmark bijection.

### Outcome decisions

- A 116/116 unique bijection permits the benchmark to claim per-shape
  reproduction and records the exact coordinate conversion; no wider census
  is authorized.
- Any missing, duplicate, or classification-disagreeing shape blocks the
  per-shape claim and is reported as a semantic/coordinate discrepancy; it
  does not authorize another radius, size, or solver campaign.

### Stop rule and finite justification

Parse each of the fixed 116 PDF pages once, derive the coordinate conversion
from Kaplan's public `kitegrid.h`, and compare canonical identities against the
existing database. Stop after one complete pass, after ten minutes of
coordinate-conversion investigation without a derivation, or before producing
10 MB of new artifacts. This finite task closes a benchmark identity question;
neither outcome calls for a larger search.
