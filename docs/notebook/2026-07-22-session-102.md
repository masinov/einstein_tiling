## Result

N21 proves that no irredundant convex polygon has a separate host side and
three or more code sides whose endpoint-angle pairs are complementary and
whose lengths sum to the host. Each code side consumes exactly `pi` exterior
turn. Several boundary blocks or one block of length at least four exceed the
polygon's total `2*pi`. One block of three consumes all curvature, forcing a
quadrilateral, but the required host-length equality makes it degenerate.

This kills right-angle ports and every convex fixed-directed-pose carrier
that admits all orders. It does not refute nonconvex supports, contextual
handedness, extra junction participants or selected non-complementary word
sets.

## Decision

No exact nonconvex polygonal witness was derived. The predeclared HC-15 stop
therefore fires after three sessions. K6O remains a precise contact-complex
search primitive; it is not a candidate tile and does not reopen K5C/K4W.

Reopening requires exact polygon coordinates and two non-reversal
host-plus-three patches with hand-checkable side recognition, disjointness
and coverage before any experiment is proposed.

No runner, enumeration, optimizer, SVG or generated research artifact was
produced.
