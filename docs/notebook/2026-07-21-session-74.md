## Question

What must be proved for a nontrivial quotient of K1C to remain decodable on
everything its finite local rules admit, rather than only on intended source
images?

## Results

**K1R (image resolving).** A finite quotient of the compact K1C system has a
finite-radius inverse on its image exactly when it is injective on complete
configurations. Compactness upgrades pointwise contextual separation to one
uniform decoding radius. Arbitrarily large globally extendable ambiguities
therefore yield a whole-plane ambiguous pair; finite samples cannot establish
resolvingness.

**K1T (local-closure totality).** For a fixed quotient presentation
`Z_rho(q)`, safety is equivalent to a bounded local decoder whose neighboring
outputs satisfy all S0 edge/vertex rules and whose re-encoding reproduces the
quotient contacts. These identities must hold on every patch admitted by
`Z_rho(q)`, not only on `q(K_full)`.

K1R is necessary but not sufficient for K1T because a two-dimensional factor
image may be strictly smaller than its finite local closure.

**N3.** Erasing every contact mode leaves the periodic triangular frame and
is unsafe. This does not refute one unmarked carrier, which may express
several contact modes through relative placements.
