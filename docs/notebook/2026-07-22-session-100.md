## Proposition

Determine whether intentional non-edge-to-edge contacts, especially several
unequal whole neighbor sides partitioning one host side, are already a
published all-tilings route from finite symbolic states to one unmarked
polygon.

## Prior art and non-redundancy

The audit reread the Spectre paper's edge-patch definition and Lemma 2.3,
then added two primary controls: Hellouin de Menibus--Lutfalla--Vanier on
symbolic-geometric rules and FLC, and Sugimoto's convex edge-to-edge periodic
boundary. The two new PDFs and text extracts are cached with hashes in
`reviews/TJUNCTION_CONTACT_COMPLEX.md`.

The edge-patch/T-junction language is already established. Arbitrary symbolic
rules can also be placed over an FLC geometric tiling, but with labels and
forbidden patterns; the purely geometric undecidability construction uses a
finite machine-dependent shapeset. No audited source supplies the narrower
one-congruence-class subdivision-order erasure with an unrestricted
all-tilings converse.

## Outcome decisions

- If a fixed subdivision word has more than one exact state under full
  Euclidean isometry and one state has an explicit nonoverlapping polygonal
  witness, retain it as a local contact kernel and state every whole-plane
  obligation still open.
- If reversal, angle closure or congruent-copy overlap collapses every state
  in the fixed class, record a scoped no-go and close the class.

Neither outcome authorizes a polygon search or an aperiodic-monotile claim.

## Stop rule and finite justification

HC-15 fixes one host interval partitioned by finitely many complete neighbor
sides. Sessions 101--102 may derive its order capacity, endpoint-angle
constraints and one exact coordinate witness or no-go. Stop after session
102. No enumeration, SAT, optimizer, SVG or candidate promotion is admitted.
