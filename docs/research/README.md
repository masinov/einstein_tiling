# Current research control plane

This directory controls current research direction. It is intentionally small
and should remain readable without consulting chronological session logs.

- [`charter.md`](charter.md) states the mission and distinguishes free
  exploration, sustained research programs, and evidence promotion.
- [`portfolio.json`](portfolio.json) records the current program-level option
  set. It is not a task queue.
- [`status.md`](status.md) gives a concise present-tense summary.
- [`ideas/`](ideas/README.md) accepts speculative material without requiring a
  research proposal.
- [`workspaces/`](workspaces/README.md) holds coherent problem-centered working
  documents rather than numbered session reports.
- [`proposals/`](proposals/README.md) is reserved for sustained commitments and
  nontrivial experiments, not ordinary proof steps.
- [`admissions/`](admissions/README.md) contains separate human authorization
  records that pin exact ready-proposal bytes.

Canonical results belong under [`../theory/`](../theory/README.md). Historical
strategy and provenance belong under the archive and chronological logs. The
research control plane links to both but does not duplicate them.
