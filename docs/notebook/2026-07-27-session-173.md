# Session 173 — exact 31-state contact compiler

## Experiment pre-registration

### Proposition

Serialize the complete rhombus-level internal contact graph of the fixed
`15,15,1` source atlas and decide the first shape-realization question:
whether macro ownership can be encoded by a single binary handedness field
whose relative value distinguishes internal from exposed contacts.  The
decision is over every address and every exact unit-edge incidence of the
three published templates, not sampled patches.

### Prior art and non-redundancy

The Akiyama--Hamada--Ito source supplies three colored patch tiles and the
edge-to-edge/SAB rule, but no common-rhombus contact table or unmarked
color-erasure construction.  The pinned source atlas in session 172 supplies
the exact finite domain.  Existing repository parity gadgets were invented
before that domain existed and are not evidence for this source-specific
compiler.

### Outcome decisions

- If the binary handedness equations are consistent, derive the resulting
  one-polygon boundary obligations and attempt an exact construction against
  the complete contact table.
- If inconsistent, emit an exact odd-cycle certificate and prove
  nonexistence for the explicitly defined binary-handedness/domain-wall
  carrier family.  Continue only with a strictly stronger compiler capable
  of representing the certified obstruction.

### Stop rule and finite justification

The domain is fixed to the 31 serialized source addresses and their unit
edges.  Compute every internal adjacency once and solve one finite parity
system.  Do not enlarge a radius, synthesize unrelated carriers, or infer a
general monotile impossibility from failure of the binary family.

### Human checkpoint

`HC-2026-07-27-42`, session distance two from the recorded baseline.  The
user has directed that checkpoint accounting remain a background safeguard;
this run directly discharges the construction/no-go obligation and creates

## Results

The exact kernel has 31 address states, 44 macro-internal full-edge contacts
(`22+22`) and 24 states incident with a macro boundary.  Every internal
contact joins different long-diagonal axes.  Nevertheless the internal graph
is not bipartite: `large_A:0`, `large_A:1`, and `large_A:2` form a triangle.
Thus even before external contacts are considered, no handedness bit can put
opposite values across every internal contact.  The stronger common-exposed
domain-wall system is also inconsistent.

## Verification

`ahi-section10-contact-kernel.json` is rebuilt exactly from the pinned atlas
by `build_sturmian_contact_kernel.py`; the independent entry point
`verify_sturmian_contact_kernel.py` passes.  The certificate records the odd
triangle explicitly.  Targeted source/contact/governance tests pass.

## Decision

The binary handedness/domain-wall carrier family is refuted.  The source
requires at least the three geometric diagonal axes and a side- or
star-context mechanism; binary variants are not pursued.
