## Results

Both large templates decompose exactly as predicted.  In each template the
three `L` rhombi form one pairwise-adjacent three-rhombus clique whose union is
a unit regular hexagon.  The six `S` rhombi split uniquely into two such
hexagons, and the other six addresses are `M` connectors.

Taking the `L`-hexagon center as root, the two unordered `S`-center vectors
(scaled by 18) are


published macros are exactly two rooted selector states.  They receive bits

needed to describe macro type.
