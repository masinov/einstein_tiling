# Session 178 — common-support retiling kernel

## Experiment pre-registration

### Proposition

Determine the exact minimum symmetric difference between the two 15-rhombus
large source patches under every full triangular-lattice isometry and
translation.  Test separately their primitive-triangle supports and their
actual SAB-rhombus dissections.  In particular, decide whether there are
congruent rhombi `R_A,R_B`, disjoint from the respective interiors, such that

```text
support(large_A) union R_A = g(support(large_B)) union R_B,
```

which would give one connected 16-rhombus region with two source-derived
retilings.

### Prior art and non-redundancy

AHI prove local interchangeable pairs at substitution scales but do not give a
common-support equality for the two optimized Figure 37 prototiles.  The exact
SER1 atlas now makes the smallest equality question finite.  This bypasses the
recognizable-edge obstruction by changing the macro partition itself rather
than decorating its boundary.

### Outcome decisions

- A one-rhombus equalizer becomes the fixed inverse-retiling carrier kernel;
  the next proof obligation is total grouping of source macros/singletons into
  that common support, followed by unrestricted periodicity and decoder gates.
- Otherwise serialize the exact best overlap and minimum number of missing
  rhombi.  Continue this regrouping route only if the minimum supplies a
  bounded source-native common-support construction, not by arbitrary padding.

### Stop rule and finite justification

Enumerate only the twelve lattice linear isometries and translations aligning
one component center or primitive vertex.  Compare finite exact sets.  Do not
search new polygon boundaries, larger superpatches, or approximate overlaps.

### Human checkpoint

`HC-2026-07-27-44`, session distance one.  This directly tests a common-support
compiler for the fixed source.

## Results

The exact maximum primitive-support overlap is `26/30` triangles.  At every
maximizer the SAB-rhombus overlap is `13/15`, so a one-rhombus equalizer does
not exist.  There are exactly four symmetry-equivalent two-rhombus
equalizers.  Their common envelope contains 34 primitive triangles (17
rhombi), is a topological disk, and has a sixteen-edge lattice boundary.

The role labels expose the remaining obstruction.  In a representative
alignment, the two cells present only in `large_A` have roles `M+S`, whereas
the two cells present only in the transformed `large_B` have roles `L+S`.
Consequently this smallest common envelope is not obtained by attaching two
copies of the published singleton `M` source tile to each large macro.  It is
an exact retiling kernel, but not yet a source-language compiler.
