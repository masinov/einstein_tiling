## ST-M1.P0

The large composition is `[12:12:6]` and the small composition is
`M=[0:2:0]`. Every non-`M` point on their segment has first/third ratio two.
Intersection with the Sturmian parabola therefore requires

`(1-beta)^2/beta^2=2`,

whose unique solution in `(0,1)` is `beta=sqrt(2)-1`.

This solution lies inside the segment. In homogeneous vector
representatives, take

`a=beta^2/6` and `b=beta(1-2 beta)`.

Both are positive, and

`a[12:12:6]+b[0:2:0]=[(1-beta)^2:2 beta(1-beta):beta^2]`.

The two large geometries can split the combined coefficient `a` arbitrarily;
their identical composition does not affect the intersection.
