# Session 48 — physical-to-parent Spectre transducer

**Date:** 2026-07-20  
**Decision:** D-0056  
**Domain:** straight `Tile(1,1)`, one handedness, rotations/translations,
edge-to-edge unit-edge tilings, with every complete physical corona in the
18-type language `L18`.

## Question

Can parent existence and unique grouping be derived from physical corona data
rather than assumed, and does the contracted parent tiling return to the
generated finite language?

## Radius-three local transducer

Starting with each of the 18 allowed central coronas, exact `L18` ring SAT
enumerates 87 radius-two and 418 radius-three rooted patches. For each patch we
enumerate every buffered exact grouping by translated/rotated occurrences of
the recovered full/missing 9/8-child templates.

All 418 cases have a parent. Raw core partitions have histogram

```text
one solution:   370
two solutions:   46
four solutions:   2
```

but every solution induces the same tile-to-parent-anchor map. The apparent
ambiguity is only whether the ninth optional child is included at the finite
boundary; the eight-child and nine-child occurrences have the same anchor.
This gives an exact 418-entry radius-three local transducer.

## Unique partition theorem inside L18

The transducer is applied independently at every tile whose complete
radius-three ball is visible. We then exhaust every physical `L18` extension
through radius six. Of the radius-five inputs, 4,482 die; the survivors produce
15,216 radius-six patches. Every survivor satisfies:

1. the central tile has one transducer anchor;
2. all eight canonical core children of that anchor are present;
3. all eight map to the same anchor.

Conversely, a tile mapped to an anchor must be a child of either the eight- or
nine-child canonical occurrence at that anchor. Hence every anchor fiber is
the common eight-child core, optionally plus the unique ninth child. Fibers
are disjoint because the transducer is a function, and they cover all tiles
because every rooted radius-three case has an anchor.

Therefore every whole-plane tiling in the stated `L18` domain has a unique
full/missing parent partition. This discharges direct-route obligations D2
and D3 only **conditional on the L18 domain**; proving that all geometric
straight-Spectre tilings enter that domain remains part of D1.

stable in level-four and level-five interiors. Exhaustive physical extension

| 7 | 10,048 | 51,309 | 6,280 | 6,280 |
| 8 | 6,199 | 0 | 1,796 | 1,796 |
| 9 | 1,735 | 0 | 4,482 | 4,482 |

At radius seven every central parent interface is complete: there are no
unresolved cases. The non-generated branch contracts sharply at radius eight
but then re-branches at radius nine. An attempted radius-ten blind expansion
was stopped after it became dominated by combinatorially irrelevant outer
ring fillings.

This is not evidence of a whole-plane counterexample: no infinite or periodic
configuration was constructed. It is decisive evidence that further physical
ring growth is the wrong proof representation. Same-language contraction D5
remains open.
