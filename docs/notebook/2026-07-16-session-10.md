## Exact adapter

- hex point `(x,y)` embeds as `(x,0,y,0)`;
- kite rotations map to even 30-degree module rotations;
- reflected kite operations map to the corresponding module reflection plus
  rotation;
- all 12 operations are pinned pointwise in regression tests.

The disk boundary is not assumed to be a supertile boundary. Every core tile
must be covered exactly once; parent candidates may use halo tiles; halo tiles
are used at most once but need not all be covered.
