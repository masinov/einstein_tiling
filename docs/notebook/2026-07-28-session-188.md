# Session 188 — complete classification of separable color erasure

## Goal-level question

Can the marked connected-stick undecidability construction be converted to
one unmarked connected polygon merely by replacing every marked unit edge by
an independent complementary jigsaw profile?

## Result

Theory note 68 proves a necessary-and-sufficient classification.  A finite
directed port relation admits such a two-participant collar erasure exactly
when its bipartite compatibility graph is a disjoint union of complete
bipartite components.  Equivalently, any two row neighborhoods are disjoint
or equal.  Positive instances have an effective rational zigzag construction.

Stade's fixed weave relation fails the criterion uniformly:

```text
R(z1,a2), R(a1,a2), R(a1,b1), not R(z1,b1).
```

Rule 9 supplies the forbidden corner, and the later input-dependent rules do
not touch the other three pairs.  Therefore no U2 instance, including the AHI
product family, can erase its complete rule relation using independent
two-body port collars on the same support.

## Meaning

This is an architecture-level unmarked no-go, not another carrier test.  It
closes every polygonal profile and every coordinate choice in the separable
two-body family.  It does not close one-unmarked-polygon realization in
general: a successful construction must use a third participant, a joint
multi-port state, carrier/verifier fusion, or a whole-plane theorem proving
that the missing local relation corners are globally unrealizable.

No experiment or search was written or launched.
