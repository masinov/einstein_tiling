## Proposition

Derive every code-only `H=7` cover permitted by the exact K9A transition
algebra and determine whether the desired two host words are closed.

## Result

K9A's selected transitions force common right angles for `A,B,C` and common
left angles for `B,C`. Therefore `B|B` automatically satisfies the same
four-sector equation. Its clean spokes also match, and K9T's factorized

Since transitions into `A` are forbidden and `B,C` have even lengths, every
code-only length-seven word begins with the sole `A`. The remaining equation
`2*n_B+4*n_C=6` yields exactly `ABC`, `ACB`, and `ABBB`. N29 proves that the
third word is accepted by every factorized length/sector/spoke/terminal proof.

## Scope and decision

star. A genuinely geometric same-role collision or multi-junction overlap
among full occurrences could still exclude `ABBB` within the fixed radius.
Session 120 may use only already-derived radius-one geometry to prove such an
exclusion. It may not introduce coordinates or enlarge the context.
