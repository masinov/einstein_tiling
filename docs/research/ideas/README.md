# Research ideas

This is a permissive, non-authoritative space for mathematical creativity.
Ideas may be incomplete, speculative, mistaken, mutually inconsistent, or
unconnected to a current program when first recorded.

An idea needs no formal proposal. A useful minimal note is:

```text
Question:
Intuition or analogy:
Possible connection to the Einstein problem:
Obvious risks or counterexamples:
```

Files here do not establish claims, authorize nontrivial computation, promote
candidates, or determine the next research direction. When an idea matures
into sustained work, its thesis and mission-level connection are recorded in
the research portfolio or a separately authorized proposal. Disposable
scratch work need not be committed at all.
