## Generic coupled CSP

The S3-specific potential encoder was generalized to exact finite-group
multiplication and inverse tables. Regression pins show that its S3 clauses
are byte-for-byte identical to the specialized encoder; a periodic one-kite
A4 control remains SAT. A4 has 48 ordered commuting twist pairs.

The 12 S3-surviving index-50 HNFs crossed with 48 A4 maps give 576 logical
pairs. Exact diagonal D6 covariance reduces them to 48 pair-orbit
representatives. Search proceeds in synchronized twist rounds: each surviving
pair orbit is tested on the same next commuting twist, and an orbit is retired
as soon as it becomes SAT. This avoids making the hard identity-twist cases a
serial bottleneck while still exhausting all 48 twists for every UNSAT orbit.

## The 16-map signature

The shared column pattern is not arbitrary. Among the 48 strong inner classes,
the killers are *exactly* those whose final three generator images are three
distinct elements of the normal Klein four subgroup `V4 < A4`. Their induced
`A4/V4 = C3` signatures are the inverse pair
`(1,2,1,0,0,0)` and `(2,1,2,0,0,0)`. The 16 classes form two complete D6 map
orbits, of sizes 12 and four.

This is an exact classification of the finite index-50 data, not yet a theorem
for an infinite HNF family. It gives the next symbolic target: derive directly

## Independent proof gate

For each of the 12 HNFs, the lowest killing class (map 7) was fixed and every
one of its 48 commuting twists was encoded separately. Glucose 4 produced 576
UNSAT proofs. During production, `drat-trim` verified the raw proof, extracted
a core CNF/core proof pair, reverified it, and a multiplicity-aware check
confirmed that every core clause came from the generated canonical CNF.

The standalone cold verifier then checked that the manifest is exactly the
full 12×48 product; verified source, search, compressed and uncompressed
hashes; regenerated every canonical CNF; repeated the core-subset test; and
replayed all 576 stored DRAT proofs with 24 independent workers. Result:
576/576 VERIFIED. The compressed payload is 2,166,298,658 bytes.
