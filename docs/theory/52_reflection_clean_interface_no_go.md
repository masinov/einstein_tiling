# Clean reflection-interface no-go

**Date:** 2026-07-27

**Status:** HC-39 exact scoped no-go; no computation

## Result

K43I reduces an occurrence-exchanging involution to half-turn or reflection.
For reflection, K43R forces every connected non-axis invariant spine to cross
the mirror once, in the interior of a side perpendicular to it.  N48 observes
that reflection preserves the two half-planes of this perpendicular side, so
the two congruent interiors locally coincide rather than separate.

Therefore a clean two-copy reflection spine must lie on the mirror axis.  An
irredundant polygon contributes only one maximal side on that line, which
cannot encode the required ordered `A,B,C,H,C,B,A` role language.

## Scope boundary

The theorem does not rule out reflection contacts with:

- a third occurrence at the fixed locus;
- a T-junction or proper side subdivision;
- several disconnected common-boundary components; or
- a context-dependent partial interface.

Each changes the full-side/clean-interface hypothesis `F` and requires a
complete finite contact language.  None is a coordinate variation of the
closed route.
