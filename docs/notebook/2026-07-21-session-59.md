# Session 59 — 2026-07-21 — W2 abelian-invariant prior-art audit

## Scope

Adversarially audit the last plausible W2 novelty claim, T2.C1/T2.C5, before
allowing any further mathematical development or computation. This was a
reading and proof-comparison session. No research runner or experiment was
written or launched.

## Primary sources checked

  especially Section 5, Theorems 5.1--5.2.
- Lidjan--Baralic, *Homology of Polyomino Tilings on Flat Surfaces*, especially

  projection proof.

Targeted searches on 2026-07-21 combined the Turtle/`Tile(sqrt(3),1)` names
with parity, coloring, torus, quotient and homology terms. They found the
known hierarchy, deformation, Ammann-bar, rhombille-coloring and Sturmian
proofs, but not the repository's exact thin-support formula. This is recorded
as a bounded absence search, not proof of global novelty.

## Result

For a quotient incidence matrix `M`, W2's witness `w^T M=0` and
`w^T 1!=0` is precisely an additive cell weighting that annihilates every tile
placement but not the target. Conway--Lagarias place this entire class inside
generalized coloring/tile homology, and flat-surface work already applies the
same logic on finite tori.

The explicit even/odd Turtle support and its D6 transport were not found in
the audited Turtle sources. But their conclusion excludes only three thin
families of periodic quotients, whereas published Turtle aperiodicity excludes
all periodic quotients. The result is therefore a worked control and compact
certificate example, not a substantive new Turtle theorem or new algebraic
method.

## Decision

Close W2 as a novelty branch. Preserve the formulas, small verifier and exact
artifacts for reproducibility; do not extend quotient indices, coefficient
fields or finite groups. The next task is a paper-only go/no-go specification
for treating the blind Hat/Turtle rediscovery as a classified-corpus benchmark.

This is session +2 under checkpoint `HC-2026-07-21-01`; it created no new
research artifact payload.
