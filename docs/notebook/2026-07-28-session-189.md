# Session 189 — Stade physical-contact quotient

## Experiment pre-registration

### Proposition

Reconstruct the labelled length-`n` Stade stick exactly as a row of regular
hexagons and, for each `n=5,...,12`, enumerate every orientation-preserving
endpoint-aligned full-edge placement of two copies.  The run will determine
the physical nonoverlap relation `G_n` and combine it with fixed weave rules
1--11.  Let `A_n=G_n intersect R` be the physically possible allowed pairs and
`F_n=G_n minus R` the physically possible forbidden pairs.  Exact separable
erasure exists precisely when completing each connected component of `A_n`
to its forced biclique introduces no member of `F_n`.  The run will decide
this graph-sandwich condition and test whether the four N61S ports remain a
physical obstruction.

The finite result is a discovery/control artifact.  Any claim for all `n>=5`
requires a separate symbolic proof from the affine hex-cell placement formula;
the eight tested lengths alone will not be promoted as that theorem.

### Prior art and non-redundancy

### Outcome decisions

- If no component completion hits `F_n`, preserve the exact component tables
  and prove the affine all-`n` statement before constructing shallow rational
  profiles.  Extra profile matches at pairs outside `G_n` are harmless because
  the carrier bodies overlap there.  Then instantiate the input-dependent AHI
  rule layer and attack full tiling-language equivalence.
- If a forced component completion hits `F_n`, preserve a shortest alternating
  allowed-contact path joining the endpoints of a physically possible
  forbidden pair.  Prove its nonoverlap for the complete all-`n` range
  symbolically, then determine whether its allowed contacts extend to
  whole-plane weave tilings.  A globally extensible witness closes separable
  erasure at the tiling-language level and becomes the exact target for a
  third-participant verifier.
- If the pattern changes with `n`, stop and derive the finite threshold cases;
  do not extend the sampled range.

### Stop rule and finite justification

Enumerate exactly all `(4n+2)^2` directed port pairs for the eight fixed
lengths `5<=n<=12`, once.  Each placement is decided by finite intersection of
two length-`n` hex-cell sets and independently checked by exact rational
polygon segment/interior predicates.  Stop after the eight relations, their
allowed-component closures and any forbidden-hit witnesses are serialized.
No radius, length, solver or
time escalation is authorized; the subsequent all-`n` result must be a proof.

family.  This closure permits arbitrary extra profile matches and accounts for
physical carrier overlap; its conclusion is an explicit periodic tiling, not
