## Fixed radius

point of its closed `H` side, including point-only participants at internal
subdivisions and endpoints. It records only contacts and sectors based on
`H`; neighbors reached solely through a participant away from `H` are not
visible. This is the complete HC-21 radius and will not be enlarged.

patches does not establish completeness on the full local closure.
