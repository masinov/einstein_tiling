## Verified conclusion

The answer is **no** under the current proposition. Local patch exhaustion,
overlap pruning, unique parent grouping, finite-state refinement, and
all-level hierarchy are already controlling features of the published
Spectre and general substitution literature. W3's cold-verifier discipline is
useful, but one known system plus no generic soundness theorem does not
establish a new method.

The remaining 80 contexts are states of an abstract over-approximation. Their
elimination would complete an independent executable reconstruction of a
known theorem, not presently produce a novel mathematical result. No further
radius/context run is authorized.
