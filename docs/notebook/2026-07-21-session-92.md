# Session 92 — the binary plaquette no-go and finite-radius survival

**Date:** 2026-07-21

**Checkpoint:** `HC-2026-07-21-12`, session 2 of 3

**Mode:** primary-source theorem audit; no experiment

## Result

Hu--Lin's corner-coloring theorem exactly settles the natural K3B rule class.
Every nonempty binary language specified by allowed `2x2` square blocks has a
doubly periodic configuration.  Therefore no such language can have a total
period-reflecting decoder to the aperiodic S0 system.  The immediate B0 target
is refuted; enumerating its 65,536 possible rule sets would have repeated a
published classification.

The broader binary route survives.  Kari--Moutot Theorem 9 and Lemma 25
construct, from any Wang system, a binary rectangular language whose complete
local closure consists exactly of translates of sparse encodings of valid
Wang tilings and whose periodic points exist exactly when the source has one.
Their Corollary 12 gives strongly aperiodic binary systems even at fixed height
two, for sufficiently large width.

## Verification

- matched B0's four macrocell bits to Hu--Lin's definition of two-color
  corner patterns, not merely to their separate edge-colored Wang result;
- checked Theorem 2.3's conclusion `Sigma(B_c) nonempty => P(B_c) nonempty`
  in its corner-coloring section;
- checked Kari--Moutot's exact equality for the full allowed closure in Lemma
  25, not only Theorem 9's nonemptiness statement;
- checked that their construction permits `m=2` and records an explicit but
  huge `n>=3(2^t-1)` bound after checkerboard doubling;
- kept Jeandel--Rao's 11-tile/four-color theorem in its correct ordinary-Wang
  scope;
- no finite census, runner or shape search was used.
