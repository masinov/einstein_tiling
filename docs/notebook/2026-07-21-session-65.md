## Primitive support derivation

At equal corridor width, every cabinet rectangle becomes one parallelogram;
in the isometric form it is the `60/120` rhombus of the trigonal lattice.
Definition 4 already splits every `S` and `L` rhombus along the distinguished
diagonal. Splitting `M` along the same marked diagonal is consistent with the
paper's use of `2M`. All primitive supports are therefore congruent
equilateral triangles.

Section 10.1 makes both `S` and `L` into hexagonal patches of six triangular
cells. Each of the two connected large templates has composition `2S+L` and
therefore 18 primitive cells; the small `M` diamond has two. The raw
macro-address alphabet is consequently `18+18+2=38`, before handedness, SAB,
orientation, and collar refinements.

## Language transport

The source system is edge-to-edge FLC. Take its finite subdivided one-corona
atlas and transport macro type, handedness, primitive address, `S/M/L` type,
the `M` half, SAB continuation, and vertex-star state to the common triangular
cellulation. Overlap-consistent higher-block colors prevent equidistancing
from adding new edge or vertex cycles. The S0C internal-address compiler then
groups every colored tiling uniquely into the original three combinatorial
macro templates.

This is a combinatorial language equivalence. No bounded Euclidean
displacement or entropy-preserving shape conjugacy is claimed.

## Minimal aperiodicity

The colors retain the virtual long/short corridor symbols. Both large classes
contain `2S+L`; the small class contains only `M`. Thus a periodic fundamental
domain has `S:L=2:1`. The source frequencies give
`(1-beta)^2/beta^2=2`, whose unique solution in `(0,1)` is
`beta=sqrt(2)-1`; the all-`M` endpoint is not on the source parabola.

A translational period of the colored triangular tiling is a nonzero vector
of the triangular lattice. It shifts at least one indexed corridor family by
a nonzero integer while preserving its color word. That word would be
periodic and hence have rational slope, a contradiction. Existence follows by
transporting the exhibited Section 10.1 tiling.

Thus E-infinity and ST-M1.S0 close in proof draft for the minimal colored
aperiodicity target.
