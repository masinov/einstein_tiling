## Recursive probe

The forced 315-anchor parent patch does not obey the Spectre simplification:
no single full-plus-one-deletion rule among exact nearest clusters of sizes
6–12 covers even the next safe core. Allowing arbitrary nearest 7/8 groups
does cover, but attempts to make the selected root/group size a deterministic
function of radius-1 through radius-4 geometry-only collar state are UNSAT.
Freezing an arbitrary full/A/B ownership assignment also fails. The successful
state abstraction is the cover-invariant option set at each forced anchor:
`[full]`, `[full,A]`, or `[full,B]`.

On the 430-parent training core, MaxSAT minimizes the next rule language to 15
typed patterns, all of arity 7. When restricted to that library, exact SAT
finds 27 selectable groups touching the 141-parent inner core, and every one
is forced; there are zero optional alternatives. The artifact records all 15
exact pose/state patterns and the 27 distinct forced grandparent anchors.
