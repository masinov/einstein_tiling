## Done

1. **Anchor hunt.** Myers' census can't anchor A2 (he states his tiling
   optimizations are incompatible with Heesch computation). Kaplan's
   "Heesch Numbers of Unmarked Polyforms" (arXiv:2105.09438 + dataset at
   cs.uwaterloo.ca/~csk/heesch/) provides the *definitions* (H_c hole-free
   convention) and its paper tables cover only polyominoes/hexes/iamonds. We
   incorrectly inferred that polykite Heesch data were uncharted; Kaplan's
   later public implementation and artifacts already cover them.
   Validation instead uses a sharp internal prediction: A1 (Myers-validated)
   says the only n≤8 tilers are the 177 periodic shapes + the hat, so every
   other A1 survivor must have finite H_c, and only the hat may grow
   indefinitely.
2. **Substrate addition:** `cells_at_point` (6/3/4 kites at center/vertex/
   midpoint, angle sums verified).
3. **A2 engine** (`src/einstein/funnel/a2_heesch.py`): corona = congruent
   copies touching the patch, covering every vertex-adjacent cell, disjoint,
   hole-free at every level (H_c). Nested DFS over corona solutions
   (exact-cover-with-optionals, min-options heuristic), global node budget.
   Verdicts: reached-cap (certificate) / exact (exhaustion) / unknown
   (budget). Certificates independently re-verified (congruence via
   canonical forms + coverage + disjointness + hole check); tamper-tested.

## Results

- **Batch n≤8** (1,087 A1 survivors, cap 2, 100k nodes): 97 s. Escalation of
  the 8 deep shapes (cap 4, 2M nodes): ~4 min.
- **Full H_c census** (all exact by exhaustion except the hat):

  | n | H=0 | H=1 | H=2 | grows past cap 4 |
  |---|----|----|----|----|
  | 2 | 1 | — | — | — |
  | 4 | 4 | 1 | — | — |
  | 5 | 12 | 14 | — | — |
  | 6 | 13 | 1 | — | — |
  | 7 | 165 | 41 | 1 | — |
  | 8 | 720 | 108 | 5 | **1 — the hat** |

- **The hat is the unique anomaly among all 1,264 free polykites n ≤ 8**:
  every other A1 survivor is exhaustively refuted at depth ≤ 2; the hat
  reaches depth 4 in 1.4 s and would reach any cap (it tiles). This is a
  mini-E1: with Heesch depth as the only signal, the funnel ranks the hat
  #1 at its size class — consistent with the program's central bet.
- First H_c=1 polykite at n=4; first H_c=2 at n=7 (unique); six H_c=2 at
  n≤8 total. Gallery renders:
  `docs/notebook/assets/deep-heesch-id{238,483,502,580,637,1080}-h2.svg`,
  `deep-heesch-id635-h4.svg` (the hat). These independently reproduce
  Kaplan's public eight-kite aggregate, rather than constituting novel census
  data.
- The unique non-tiling 2-kite has H_c = 0.
