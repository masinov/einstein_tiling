## Result

The W3 recognisability clause is now split into two proof routes with different
logical roles.

Walton's Theorem 5.2 gives unique composition modulo translation for compact
Hausdorff expansive `L`-sub pattern spaces. Here `L`-sub includes a surjective
local-derivation subdivision on the whole selected pattern space. For
return-discrete tiling spaces, Corollary 5.5 says strict injectivity is
equivalent to the absence of periodic hull elements. It therefore cannot be
used circularly as the independent proof of that absence.

Chéritat's Corollary 63 is the direct control. Its domain is every whole-plane
undeformed `Tile(1,1)` tiling using rotations and translations but no
reflections. A finite local-case chain passes through decorated components,
interfaces, triangular pieces and packs; every grouping and correspondence is
unique, and the result is a reflected parent system of the same kind. The
argument iterates. Proposition 64 and Corollary 65 separately reverse the
construction to establish existence.
