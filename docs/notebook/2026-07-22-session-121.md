# Session 121 — finite weighted host-language criterion

**Date:** 2026-07-22

**Checkpoint:** `HC-2026-07-22-22`, session 1 of 3

**Mode:** on-paper arithmetic language theorem; no experiment

## Proposition

Treat selected words and side lengths jointly and reduce uniqueness of a
host subdivision language to finite exact arithmetic.

## Result

K13W represents a design by a finite directed transition graph, start/end
classes, exact positive role weights, host length and desired words. The
transition graph is the closure forced by local equations, not the desired
adjacency list. If `delta` is the smallest role length, every host word has at
most `floor(h/delta)` roles. Exact enumeration of those bounded paths is
therefore necessary and sufficient for language equality.

For rational weights the test is a finite integer dynamic program; fixed
algebraic-number fields remain finite by exact equality. No run is needed for
the three-role K9A specialization.

## Scope and decision

This is a standard weighted-automaton theorem and carries no method-novelty
claim. It does not establish geometric realizability, full-local-closure
completeness, tiling or aperiodicity. Sessions 122--123 must derive a closed
arithmetic characterization for the jointly forced `ABC/ACB` language and
prove either a reusable family or nonexistence.

## Verification

- Positivity gives the exact finite bound `k*delta<=h`.
- Cycles do not threaten finiteness below a fixed path length.
- No computation or artifact was generated.
