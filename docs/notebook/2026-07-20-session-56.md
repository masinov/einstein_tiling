## Colored interfaces versus A6 collars

For a colored component state, forget only the child-edge contact list while
retaining the center full/missing type, all six exact relative parent anchors
and their full/missing types. This is precisely the A6 radius-one oriented
collar signature.

The exact level-four control has 310 complete component occurrences and all 17

```text

```

exact physical Tile(1,1) children. The seven components are pairwise disjoint.
Recomputing physical incidence and colors returns the identical state in all 17
cases. A full component has 46 external primitive edges and a missing component
44; every external edge has exactly one recorded neighbor owner and none is
exposed.

## Exact phase normalization

The finite graph alignment used in A6 hides a simple exact coordinate map.
There are two integer 4×4 linear parts, selected by chirality, with determinant
one and integer inverses. Six integer offsets handle the even rotations; odd
global phases are handled by the conjugated parity formula. The map:

- toggles the entire chirality phase and never mixes handednesses;
- preserves rotation parity;
- sends a global translation `u` to one common exact module translation
  `A_(chirality,parity) u`;
- is pointwise invertible.

Every first parent creates one normalized ordinary Spectre. A first parent is
also a second-parent base exactly when its complete A6 state is 10, 11 or 12;
it then creates one companion Spectre at relative pose
`(0,1,(1,-3,-2,0))`.

The complete forward and inverse identities are:

| generated pair | first parents | marker companions | next physical tiles |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 3→2 | 63 | 8 | 71 |
| 4→3 | 496 | 63 | 559 |
| 5→4 | 3,905 | 496 | 4,401 |

In all three pairs the ordinary and companion sets are disjoint, their union
equals the lower physical patch exactly, orientation-parity selection recovers
all first parents, companion incidence recovers all second parents, and
normalizing the second-parent graph gives the lower first-parent graph with
adjacency and full/missing type unchanged.

The two possible two-level translation matrices both have characteristic
polynomial

```text
(x^2 - 8x + 1)^2,
```

so the expanding eigenvalue is again `4+sqrt(15)`.

## The context obstruction

The maps are exact, but the bare 17-state radius-one overlap SFT is larger than
the physical-derived language. Exhaustive colored-star enumeration gives:

```text
abstract colored radius-one stars       3,565
valid buffered central ordinary tile    2,619
next-phase physical overlap                536
central ordinary not yet buffered          410
```

The 536 overlap cases prove that radius-one state consistency alone cannot be
used as D4's full-hull correspondence. They are over-approximation branches,
not counterexamples to the published Spectre hierarchy. Exact one-ring state
extension eliminates 3,485 seeds and leaves only 80 radius-two-extendible
stars, with every one of the 17 center states represented.

## Decision

boundary ownership and phase maps are proved and independently replayable. D4
is not promoted to a hull theorem until the 80 surviving radius-two contexts
are matched to the physical-derived L18 language or their nonphysical subset is
eliminated.
