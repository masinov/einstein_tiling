## Representation

For n≤16, one canonical shape is a fixed 32-byte key of sixteen `u16` slots.
Each occupied slot packs:

- translated center x/2 in 6 bits;
- translated center y/2 plus 32 in 6 bits;
- kite orientation in 3 bits.

Unused slots are `0xffff`. Canonicalization applies the same twelve kite-grid
operations as Python, translates the lexicographically first transformed cell
to the origin, sorts, and selects the minimum key. The coordinate bounds are
asserted during every run.

The binary stream starts with magic `A0PK`, version, cell count and record
count, followed by fixed-width canonical records. It is deliberately
unordered: downstream stages must not attach semantics to hash-table order.
